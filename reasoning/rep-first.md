## Requirements and primary authorities

- [RPC 1.2, 1.4 and 4.1](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx) support accurate notice within actual authority.
- [§ 38.2-2206](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title38.2/chapter22/section38.2-2206/) may apply to UM/UIM coverage; it does not make every first-party claim a UM claim.

## Conditional decisions

- Do not promise that routing communications to counsel suspends cooperation duties or policy deadlines.
- A demand for documents is a request unless a specific entitlement is established.

## Presentation choices

Black 12-point Times New Roman, one-inch margins and single spacing. Block letter format with paragraph gaps.

## Required case inputs

Insured status, insurer, policy period, incident, known coverage, actual claim number, contact and any pending carrier requests.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from the cited requirements; no commercial sample copied. Do not invent consent, service, signatures, facts, attachments, dates or completed transactions.
