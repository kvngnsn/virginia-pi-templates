## Requirements and primary authorities

- [§ 8.01-264](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-264/) governs objection timing and the proposed proper forum.
- [§ 8.01-265](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-265/) addresses good-cause transfer, including preferred-venue limits.

## Conditional decisions

- Do not conflate an improper-venue objection with discretionary transfer or out-of-state forum non conveniens.
- Verify §§ 8.01-261/262 for the actual action and preserve separate process objections if relevant.
