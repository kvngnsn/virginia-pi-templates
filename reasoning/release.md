# Release of Identified Personal Injury Claims reasoning

**Verified September 21, 2026.** Scope: Adult claimant’s defined release in an ordinary private PI settlement; no automatic universal release of all persons or all unrelated claims.

Read [shared decisions](common.md) alongside this file.

## Requirements and primary authorities

- [§ 8.01-35.1(A), (D)](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title8.01/chapter3/section8.01-35.1/) addresses release effects and cross-references protected settlements.
- [§ 38.2-2206(K)–(L)](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title38.2/chapter22/section38.2-2206/) requires special terms and execution for that UIM settlement route; this generic release is not itself that complete instrument.

## Conditional decisions

- Unknown-injury coverage, indemnity, reservations and effectiveness are express client-approved choices.
- Do not add notarization as a universal requirement or claim signed authority/receipt before it exists.
- Minors, incapacitated claimants, estates and UIM statutory settlements require tailored execution and approval.

## Presentation choices

Single-spaced plain document with a descriptive title, short labeled sections and blank execution fields. No court caption or law-firm letter salutation; this is a readability choice, not a prescribed form.

## Required case inputs

Actual consideration, payment trigger, exact released parties/claims, client’s unknown-injury decision, reserved rights, lien terms and authorized signatories.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from the cited requirements; no commercial sample copied. Do not invent consent, service, signatures, facts, attachments, dates or completed transactions.
