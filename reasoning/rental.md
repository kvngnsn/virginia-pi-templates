## Requirements and primary authorities

- [§ 8.01-66](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-66/) distinguishes actual reasonable rental loss from the conditional bad-faith remedy in subsection B.

## Conditional decisions

- Do not compute a fictional daily rental expense where no rental cost was incurred.
- Policy rental benefits and third-party tort damages require separate bases; deduct prior payments once.
