# Notice of Representation to Liability Insurer

Template: [rep-adverse.md](../templates/insurance/rep-adverse.md) · Word: [rep-adverse.docx](../templates/insurance/docx/rep-adverse.docx) · Shared choices: [common.md](common.md)

Verified: September 21, 2026.

**Scope:** Virginia personal-injury matter; ordinary adult claimant.

## Requirements and authorities

- An attorney must give written notice of representation before invoking the pre-suit motor-vehicle disclosure mechanism. [§ 8.01-417(C)](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title8.01/chapter14/section8.01-417/).

## Conditional rules and case decisions

- This notice is not itself a complete statutory policy-limits request. Do not claim the insurer has a statutory response deadline for this acknowledgment.
- Representation, insured identity and authority to disclose client information must be confirmed; no lien, assignment or settlement authority is created.

## Presentation decisions

An ordinary block letter with a concise claim-identification paragraph. There is no identified statewide pleading-format mandate for this letter.

See [shared formatting decisions](common.md): court requirements, observed caption convention and house style are separate.

## Required case inputs

Client, represented claim, loss date/location, insured, insurer, adjuster and claim number if known. Counsel and firm contact details.

## Use boundary

An editable drafting starting point. No facts, signature, completed service, consent, judicial ruling or attachment is established by a placeholder.
