# Authorized Prior Claims History Request reasoning

**Verified September 21, 2026.** Scope: Request to a verified claims-data custodian or insurer through a lawful individual-access or authorized-disclosure route.

Read [shared decisions](common.md) alongside this file.

## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Virginia RPC 1.6 and 4.1](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx): require protection of client information and truthful representations of authority.

## Conditional decisions

- ISO/AISG in the catalog describes possible industry sources; it grants no license or right to query a database.
- Verify the actual custodian, authorization and applicable privacy/consumer-reporting route. Do not assert FCRA entitlement unless the particular report and provider qualify.
- No unsupported fixed statutory response deadline is included.

## Presentation choices

Single-spaced block letter with paragraph spacing and a compact signature. No court caption or ornamental rules. These are presentation choices, not a prescribed form.

## Required case inputs

Actual custodian/report product, client authorization, lawful access basis, relevant period, identifiers through secure channel and fee cap.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from requirements, not copied from a commercial form. No facts, signatures, service, consent, notarization, attachments, receipt of money or judicial acts may be invented.
