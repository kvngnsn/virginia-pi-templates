# Settlement Counteroffer

Template: [counter.md](../templates/demand/counter.md) · Word: [counter.docx](../templates/demand/docx/counter.docx) · Shared choices: [common.md](common.md)

Verified: September 21, 2026.

**Scope:** Virginia personal-injury matter; ordinary adult claimant.

## Requirements and authorities

- Counsel must respect the client’s settlement decision. [Virginia RPC 1.2(a)](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx).
- [Rule 2:408](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) governs certain evidentiary uses, not all disclosure or enforceability questions.

## Conditional rules and case decisions

- Confirm whether the communication rejects a prior offer and whether any offer remains open; never insert a universal reservation of an offer’s legal effect.
- No payment, lien satisfaction, confidentiality, release or client authorization is assumed.

## Presentation decisions

One concise letter that identifies the prior offer, current amount and material terms. No court styling or automatic “privileged” banner.

See [shared formatting decisions](common.md): court requirements, observed caption convention and house style are separate.

## Required case inputs

Actual prior offer, client authority, counteroffer amount, material terms, record-supported rationale, recipient and requested response date.

## Use boundary

An editable drafting starting point. No facts, signature, completed service, consent, judicial ruling or attachment is established by a placeholder.
