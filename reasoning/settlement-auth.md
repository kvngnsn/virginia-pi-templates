# Client Settlement Authorization reasoning

**Verified September 21, 2026.** Scope: Competent adult client’s specific settlement instruction; not a release to the opposing party or court approval for a protected claimant.

Read [shared decisions](common.md) alongside this file.

## Requirements and primary authorities

- [RPC 1.2(a) and 1.4](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx) reserve the settlement decision to the client and require sufficient explanation of material terms.
- [RPC 1.5(c) and 1.15](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx) inform fee explanation and treatment of settlement funds.

## Conditional decisions

- Do not treat a signed authorization as an executed release or proof that money was received.
- Minors, incapacitated persons, wrongful-death estates, aggregate settlements and UM/UIM issues require additional authority and procedures.

## Presentation choices

Single-spaced plain document with a descriptive title, short labeled sections and blank execution fields. No court caption or law-firm letter salutation; this is a readability choice, not a prescribed form.

## Required case inputs

Exact offer and release version, client authority/capacity, fee calculation, supported expense/lien figures, net estimate and explicit client decision.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from the cited requirements; no commercial sample copied. Do not invent consent, service, signatures, facts, attachments, dates or completed transactions.
