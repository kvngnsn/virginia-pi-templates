## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Virginia Code § 8.01-383.1](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-383.1/): subsection A preserves election and acceptance under protest; additur is a different subsection.
- [Allstate Insurance Co. v. Wade, 265 Va. 383, 394–95 (2003)](https://www.vacourts.gov/opinions/opnscvwp/1021201.pdf): discusses excessive awards and the evidentiary record.
- [Rule 1:1](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf): controls timely trial-court authority.

## Conditional decisions

- Do not simply replace the jury’s award with counsel’s preferred figure.
- Special caps and punitive-damages review require separate authority when applicable.
