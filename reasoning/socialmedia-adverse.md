## Requirements and primary authorities

- [§ 8.01-379.2:1](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-379.2:1/) supplies the preservation framework.
- [Rules 4:1(b), 4:1(c) and 4:9](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) govern relevance, protection and later production.
- [Rules 3.4(a) and 4.2](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx) apply to evidence and represented recipients.

## Conditional decisions

- Tie accounts and dates to case issues; no blanket demand for every private message.
- Preservation is distinct from compelled disclosure.

## Presentation choices
