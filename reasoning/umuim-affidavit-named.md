## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Virginia Code § 38.2-2206](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/38.2-2206/): governs uninsured/underinsured coverage, unknown motorists, reporting, certification and service.

## Conditional decisions

- A named driver’s affidavit is not conclusive proof that no policy or self-insurance applies. DMV certification under subsection C has a distinct statutory effect.
- Use the actual knowledgeable affiant. Formal UM-carrier / John Doe service and policy requirements remain separate; this affidavit does not satisfy all of them.
