## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Rules 4:1(b)(6), (e), (g), 4:8, 4:9 and 4:11](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) distinguish privilege descriptions, supplementation, signature, sworn interrogatory answers, production withholding and admission responses.

## Conditional decisions

- Do not insert boilerplate objections to every request. Match the actual type, deadline, burden facts and preservation/waiver rules.
- If interrogatory answers are supplied, append a valid oath/verification and actual execution; the text does not claim a signature occurred.
- Do not conflate inability to admit after reasonable inquiry with a privilege objection or a general denial.

## Presentation choices

Borderless parenthesis caption, black 12-point Times New Roman, one-inch margins, double spacing, restrained headings and page numbers; see common.md. These choices do not establish any local-court approval.

## Required case inputs

Complete served requests, originals/numbering, service dates, actual factual inquiry, narrow objection grounds, response/withholding position and verification needs.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from the cited requirements; no commercial sample copied. Do not invent consent, service, signatures, facts, attachments, dates or completed transactions.
