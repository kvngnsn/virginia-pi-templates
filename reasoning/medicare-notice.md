## Requirements and source-supported constraints

The BCRC reporting process identifies beneficiary, incident, injuries, insurer and settlement status so conditional payments can be investigated.

## Conditional decisions and boundaries

Initial occurrence reporting is distinct from proof of representation and later settlement reporting. Do not represent an unknown recovery amount as zero.
