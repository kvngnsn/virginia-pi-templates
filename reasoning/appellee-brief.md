## Requirements and primary authorities

Current [Virginia Rules of Court](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) were downloaded and compared with the existing snapshot; SHA-256 is `15a5095aba5043b863d2c7fb26f24c2556cad4908054874c0dff4f770af53a0f`. The [amendments index](https://www.vacourts.gov/courts/scv/amend) was checked for effective changes.

- Rules 5A:1(c), 5A:4 and 5A:19 control filing/service, typography, cover, signature, certification, length and schedule; Rule 5A:21 controls the responsive brief's contents.
- Rule 5A:21 requires contents/authorities; a case statement when disagreeing; facts necessary to correct/amplify the opening brief; the standard and argument for each assignment together; properly identified cross-errors with exact preservation references when raised; precise relief; counsel information; and service, oral-argument and word-count certification.
- Additional assignments bind the party absent permission to amend. Cross-error must not be invented merely because the skeleton contains a conditional heading. Rule 5A:18 exceptions need an actual supported basis.
- The applicable ordinary merits-brief limit is the longer of 50 pages or 12,300 words absent court permission. In an appeal of right the normal responsive deadline is 30 days after opening-brief filing or record filing, whichever is later; confirm procedural posture and any order rather than hard-coding a date.
- [Court brief instructions](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/cav/forms/formatting_instructions_briefs.pdf) govern final PDF preparation. [Publisher appellee skeleton](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/cav/forms/brief_appellee_template.pdf) is a structural cross-check; current Rule 5A:21 supplies the governing requirements.

## Conditional decisions

- Appellee responds to the appeal; trial plaintiff/defendant labels do not determine appellate roles. Scope is a Virginia Court of Appeals civil merits response from circuit court.
- Respond to every assignment raised by appellant; do not organize the entire response around nonexistent cross-error. Cross-error has a separate conditional section with preservation and argument.
- Explain only necessary disagreement or amplification of the case/facts. Verify every record reference and authority rather than importing material outside the record.
- The actual oral-argument election, count and service event remain unresolved until provided or measured.

## Presentation choices

Same appellate profile as the opening brief: approved Times New Roman 14, one-inch margins, double-spaced body, unnumbered cover, Roman table pages and Arabic body. These are distinguished from the earlier circuit-court 12-point house profile. No new styling is imposed on existing templates.

## Required case inputs

Opening brief, actual appellate record/appendix, appellate style/number, preserved cross-errors if any, court orders, issue-specific law, requested disposition, counsel details, oral-argument election, measured count and actual service.

## Verification and limits

Original clean drafting skeleton, with required sections and unresolved facts visible. Review of the blank template does not establish case-filled legal accuracy. Signatures, completed service, fees, enclosures and judicial acts must never be inferred from the request to draft.

The delivered artifact is an editable Word drafting template. Final PDF export, populated contents/authorities, bookmarks, PDF page labels, opening-view settings and the final word count still require verification before VACES filing. This library does not package a PDF filing or claim an unfilled Word skeleton is filing-ready. Keep the distinct cover, front-matter and body sections marked by `<!-- frontmatter -->` and `<!-- body -->`.
