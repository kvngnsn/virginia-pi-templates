## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Rules 2:104, 2:702–2:705](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) govern qualification, assistance, foundation and permissible opinion testimony.
- [§ 8.01-401.3](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-401.3/) and [§ 8.01-401.1](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-401.1/) govern civil expert opinions and their bases.

## Conditional decisions

- Do not import Daubert or Federal Rule 702 as the governing test merely because the command is /daubert.
- A medical-malpractice expert may implicate distinct statutory qualifications; this is a framework, not a completed specialty challenge.
