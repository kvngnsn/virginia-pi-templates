## Requirements and primary authorities

- [RPC 1.5(c)](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx) requires a written closing statement showing outcome and remittance calculation; [RPC 1.15](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx) requires proper handling and accounting for client and protected third-party funds.

## Conditional decisions

- Distinguish promised recovery, money received, cleared funds and amounts available for distribution.
- Do not double-count expenses deducted before the fee calculation or payments already made.
- Disputed or protected funds require proper handling; a client signature alone does not authorize diversion of a valid third-party lien.

## Presentation choices

Single-spaced plain document with a descriptive title, short labeled sections and blank execution fields. No court caption or law-firm letter salutation; this is a readability choice, not a prescribed form.

## Required case inputs

Actual receipts, fee agreement/stage, verified expenses/payoffs, prior distributions, disputed sums, reserves, clearance and arithmetic reconciliation.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from the cited requirements; no commercial sample copied. Do not invent consent, service, signatures, facts, attachments, dates or completed transactions.
