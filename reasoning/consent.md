## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Rule 1:13](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) governs endorsements and presentation; [RPC 1.2 and 4.1](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx) require actual authority and truthful statements about consent.

## Conditional decisions

- Identify missing consent; do not use “agreed” merely because a draft was circulated.
- Confirm the court has authority to order the requested relief and that deadlines requiring court action are actually changed by entry.

## Presentation choices

Borderless parenthesis caption, black 12-point Times New Roman, one-inch margins, double spacing, restrained headings and page numbers; see common.md. These choices do not establish any local-court approval.

## Required case inputs

Exact agreement, authorized signatories, issue, operative obligations/dates, remaining claims, costs and presentation procedure.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from the cited requirements; no commercial sample copied. Do not invent consent, service, signatures, facts, attachments, dates or completed transactions.
