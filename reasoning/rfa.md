# Plaintiff’s First Requests for Admission to Defendant

Template: [rfa.md](../templates/discovery/rfa.md) · Word: [rfa.docx](../templates/discovery/docx/rfa.docx) · Shared choices: [common.md](common.md)

Verified: September 21, 2026.

**Scope:** Virginia Circuit Court civil personal-injury litigation.

## Requirements and authorities

- [Rule 4:11(a), (e)](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf): separately stated matters; documents supplied or made available; response standards; 30 substantive requests including subparts unless agreement or leave.
- [Rule 4:11(b)](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf): admissions bind in this action, subject to permitted withdrawal/amendment.

## Conditional rules and case decisions

- Document-genuineness requests have a separate numerical rule. Do not label compound requests as single matters to evade the limit.
- Use supportable propositions and actual documents, not invented stipulated facts. Track actual response and service dates; no automatic filed certificate with made-up facts.

## Presentation decisions

Four editable starter slots, including one distinct genuineness request; no compound “admit liability and all damages” shortcut.

See [shared formatting decisions](common.md): court requirements, observed caption convention and house style are separate.

## Required case inputs

Precise facts to test, complete parties, prior request count, genuinely supplied documents and applicable deadline.

## Use boundary

An editable drafting starting point. No facts, signature, completed service, consent, judicial ruling or attachment is established by a placeholder.
