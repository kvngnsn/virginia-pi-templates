## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Virginia Code § 8.01-66.2](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-66.2/): private provider statutory lien amounts and categories differ from the underlying patient debt.
- [Virginia Code § 8.01-66.5](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-66.5/): governs compromise and statutory allocation of covered private liens.
- [Virginia Code § 8.01-66.9](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-66.9/): Commonwealth/public-program claims have a distinct framework.
- [Virginia RPC 1.15](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx): requires protection and accounting for funds in which others have qualifying interests.

## Conditional decisions

- The lienholder, not counsel for the patient without authority, signs the release.
- A statutory lien cap is not a cap on all patient debt. State the actual discharge of debt separately.
- Do not use this in place of a judgment satisfaction, government compromise or insurer-specific statutory release.

## Presentation choices

Single-spaced plain document with short labeled sections, readable execution fields and page numbers. No pleading caption. This is a house design, not a court-mandated form.

## Required case inputs

Lienholder authority, exact interest, payoff, actual payment trigger, scope, account balance, LOP terms and required formalities.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from requirements, not copied from a commercial form. No facts, signatures, service, consent, notarization, attachments, receipt of money or judicial acts may be invented.
