## Requirements and primary authorities

- [45 C.F.R. § 164.508(b)–(c)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/subtitle-A/subchapter-C/part-164/subpart-E/section-164.508) specifies the core information, discloser, recipient, purpose, expiration, signature/date, representative authority and required notices.

## Conditional decisions

- Every required field must be completed before signature/use. No indefinite expiration for an ordinary legal-records authorization.
- This deliberately excludes psychotherapy notes and Part 2 records; obtain an appropriate separate authorization for excluded categories or stricter state-law requirements.
- This is not a sale/marketing/research authorization and assumes the ordinary prohibition on conditioning treatment/benefits.

## Presentation choices

Standalone document with single spacing, restrained headings and explicit fields; no court caption. ## Required case inputs

Patient, exact record holders/recipients, meaningful scope/dates, purpose, expiry, revocation address and signer authority.
