## Requirements and source-supported constraints

Review anti-subrogation restrictions before compromise. An offer cannot be drafted on the assumption that a valid reimbursement claim necessarily exists.

## Conditional decisions and boundaries

Distinguish a commercial proposal from a legally mandatory reduction. Require client authority, valid payee instructions and a written scope of release.
