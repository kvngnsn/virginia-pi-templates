## Requirements and primary authorities

- [§ 8.01-277(A)](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-277/) permits a motion before or simultaneously with merits pleading and allows specified corrective relief.
- [§ 8.01-277.1](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-277.1/) identifies conduct that can waive process/personal-jurisdiction objections.
- [Rule 3:8(a1)–(b)](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) governs responsive sequence.

## Conditional decisions

- Select the actual service provision; a defect and a lack of personal jurisdiction are related but distinct grounds.
- Do not label an appearance special while seeking unrelated merits relief; subsection B untimely-service dismissal has different conditions.
