# Employee Safety and Exposure Records Request reasoning

**Verified September 21, 2026.** Scope: Authorized employee request to an employer for covered OSHA/VOSH records; separate voluntary requests from regulatory access rights.

Read [shared decisions](common.md) alongside this file.

## Requirements and primary authorities

- [29 C.F.R. § 1904.35](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/29/1904.35): distinguishes employee/personal-representative access from collective-bargaining-representative access.
- [29 C.F.R. § 1910.1020](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/29/1910.1020): defines covered exposure/medical records, authorization and access timing.

## Conditional decisions

- Confirm establishment coverage, recordkeeping exemptions and whether VOSH or federal OSHA regulates the site.
- Do not apply the 15-working-day provision to every employer record. First copies, additional copies and privacy-sensitive records have different rules.
- A client authorization alone does not permit counsel to access all other employees’ medical information.

## Presentation choices

Single-spaced block letter with paragraph spacing and a compact signature. No court caption or ornamental rules. These are presentation choices, not a prescribed form.

## Required case inputs

Employee, employment dates, establishment, jurisdiction, written designation, specific medical authorization and requested categories.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from requirements, not copied from a commercial form. No facts, signatures, service, consent, notarization, attachments, receipt of money or judicial acts may be invented.
