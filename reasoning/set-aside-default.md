## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Rules 3:19 and 1:1](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf): distinguish pre-judgment good cause, timely trial-court control, and later statutory relief.
- [Virginia Code § 8.01-428](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-428/): enumerates default-judgment grounds and other limited remedies.

## Conditional decisions

- Select and retain only the applicable procedural branch; do not combine incompatible standards.
- A pending motion does not extend Rule 1:1. A timely entered order, not merely a request, is necessary to suspend the judgment.
