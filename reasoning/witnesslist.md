## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Rules 1:18 and 4:13; Part One Appendix Form 3, section V](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) supply scheduling/list context when that order applies. [Rule 4:1](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) separately governs expert and discovery disclosures.

## Conditional decisions

- Use the actual order’s filing/exchange requirements and date; do not automatically apply the illustrative form’s dates to every case.
- Protect unnecessary personal information and distinguish listing, subpoena service and admissibility.

## Presentation choices

Borderless parenthesis caption, black 12-point Times New Roman, one-inch margins, double spacing, restrained headings and page numbers; see common.md. These choices do not establish any local-court approval.

## Required case inputs

Entered order, trial date, actual witness identities/contact requirements, capacities/subjects, expert disclosures and availability/designation status.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from the cited requirements; no commercial sample copied. Do not invent consent, service, signatures, facts, attachments, dates or completed transactions.
