## Requirements and source-supported constraints

Section 8.01-35.1 governs specified effects of a good-faith release or covenant in relation to other liable persons and contribution; actual terms still matter.

## Conditional decisions and boundaries

Use for an intentionally limited covenant, not a generic full release. Check UM/UIM, minors, estates, approval and third-party interests separately where implicated. Do not promise that reserved claims escape statutory reductions.
