## Requirements and primary authorities

Current [Virginia Rules of Court](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) were downloaded and compared with the existing snapshot; SHA-256 is `15a5095aba5043b863d2c7fb26f24c2556cad4908054874c0dff4f770af53a0f`. The [amendments index](https://www.vacourts.gov/courts/scv/amend) was checked for effective changes.

- [Virginia Code § 8.01-407](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-407/) requires the attorney-issued witness summons copy and sheriff service fees, when applicable, to be mailed or delivered to the clerk on the day of issuance. A sheriff-bound subpoena must also have a transmittal sheet and comply with the statute's identification, fee and timing provisions.
- Rule 4:9A(a)(2) independently requires a same-day clerk copy of an attorney-issued subpoena duces tecum with the certificate of Rule 1:12 service on counsel and unrepresented parties.
- [Fairfax clerk letter](https://www.fairfaxcounty.gov/circuit/sites/circuit/files/Assets/Documents/PDF/attorney-issued-subpoena-letter-clerk.pdf) and [sheriff sheet](https://www.fairfaxcounty.gov/circuit/sites/circuit/files/Assets/Documents/PDF/attorney-issued-subpoena-letter-sheriff.pdf) illustrate different recipients. They are local samples, not compulsory statewide wording. Neither existing original is replaced.

## Conditional decisions

- This command is scoped to the circuit court clerk cover letter. The actual subpoena and any sheriff transmittal sheet remain separate. This template does not issue process.
- Verify active Virginia Bar status, actual issuance/transmittal dates, intended server and actual fees/enclosures. Do not declare payment or delivery that has not happened.
- Do not reproduce the sheriff sample's wording asking a sheriff to “issue” an already attorney-issued subpoena; issuance and service are different acts.
- Special recipients, short-notice service, health records and nonparty records require the applicable source process. No existing official-form dispatch or PDF filler was changed.

## Presentation choices

Single-spaced conventional correspondence with sender, actual date, clerk addressee, case reference, short paragraphs, signature and enclosure inventory. The statute supplies the transmission obligations; letter appearance is a house choice.

## Required case inputs

Case style/number, actual court/clerk address, issuing attorney and bar status, each subpoena type/recipient/date, actual transmittal event, server, fee remittance and any Rule 1:12 certificate.

## Verification and limits

Original clean drafting skeleton, with required sections and unresolved facts visible. Review of the blank template does not establish case-filled legal accuracy. Signatures, completed service, fees, enclosures and judicial acts must never be inferred from the request to draft.
