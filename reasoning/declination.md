# Nonengagement Letter reasoning

Template: [declination.md](../templates/client/declination.md) · Word: [declination.docx](../templates/client/docx/declination.docx) · Shared choices: [common.md](common.md)

**Verified September 21, 2026.** Scope: Prospective adult client where representation has not begun; not withdrawal from an existing case.

Read [shared decisions](common.md) alongside this file.

## Requirements and primary authorities

- [RPC 1.18 and 1.6](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx) address protected information; nonengagement does not eliminate confidentiality duties.
- [RPC 1.16](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx) requires different steps if representation already exists.

## Conditional decisions

- Do not use a nonengagement letter to evade withdrawal or protect against a deadline already undertaken.
- A deadline warning must use the actual claim, accrual facts and governing law, including special notice regimes.

## Presentation choices

Black 12-point Times New Roman, one-inch margins and single spacing. Block letter format with paragraph gaps.

## Required case inputs

Actual review performed, representation status, reason if disclosed, verified urgent deadlines, return of client materials and delivery details.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from the cited requirements; no commercial sample copied. Do not invent consent, service, signatures, facts, attachments, dates or completed transactions.
