## Requirements and primary authorities

- [§ 46.2-1088.6(B)–(E)](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title46.2/chapter10/section46.2-1088.6/) restricts access, addresses post-event consent and preserves specified ownership rights after a nonbeneficial transfer.
- [§ 8.01-379.2:1](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title8.01/chapter13/section8.01-379.2:1/) provides the fact-dependent preservation framework.

## Conditional decisions

- An insurer, salvage custodian or prospective buyer does not automatically have authority to consent for the statutory owner.
- Destructive testing, protected third-party information and court-ordered access require an express protocol or order.
