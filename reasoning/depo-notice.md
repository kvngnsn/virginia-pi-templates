# Notice of Deposition

Template: [depo-notice.md](../templates/discovery/depo-notice.md) · Word: [depo-notice.docx](../templates/discovery/docx/depo-notice.docx) · Shared choices: [common.md](common.md)

Verified: September 21, 2026.

**Scope:** Notice of an individual’s deposition in Virginia Circuit Court civil litigation; excludes organization-topic and subpoena forms.

## Requirements and authorities

- [Rule 4:5(a), (b)(1), (b)(7), (c)](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf): reasonable written notice to all parties; deponent identity/address, time/place; appropriate officer in the deponent’s locality for remote examination.

## Conditional rules and case decisions

- A notice is not a subpoena compelling a nonparty. Check leave requirements, witness status, service and other court orders.
- An organizational deposition needs particularized topics and conferral under Rule 4:5(b)(6); this individual-deponent template is not a replacement. Party document requests use Rule 4:9 timing.

## Presentation decisions

Deposition logistics are grouped in readable paragraphs. Remote method and recording are separate fields; using Zoom does not imply permission for every recording method.

See [shared formatting decisions](common.md): court requirements, observed caption convention and house style are separate.

## Required case inputs

Deponent status/name/address, confirmed date/time/time zone, physical location, remote access, officer, method, leave/order and actual service.

## Use boundary

An editable drafting starting point. No facts, signature, completed service, consent, judicial ruling or attachment is established by a placeholder.
