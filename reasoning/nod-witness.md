## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Rule 4:5(a), (a1)(ii)–(iv), (b)(1)](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) distinguishes notice from compulsory attendance, regulates nonparty locations and addresses out-of-state procedures.

## Conditional decisions

- Virginia notice does not itself exercise another state’s subpoena power.
- Use the actual witness agreement or required process; do not invent completed service or attendance consent.
