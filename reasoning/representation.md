## Requirements and primary authorities

- [RPC 1.2 and 1.4](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx) govern scope, client settlement decisions and material communications.
- [RPC 1.5](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx) governs explaining fees; this confirmation refers to the actual agreement rather than inventing new terms.

## Conditional decisions

- Send only when representation is actually established; use declination for a prospective matter the firm will not undertake.
- Limited scope and excluded appeals require an appropriate actual agreement.

## Presentation choices

Black 12-point Times New Roman, one-inch margins and single spacing. Block letter format with paragraph gaps.

## Required case inputs

Client, signed or otherwise established engagement, scope, exclusions, responsible lawyer, communication method and immediate tasks.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from the cited requirements; no commercial sample copied. Do not invent consent, service, signatures, facts, attachments, dates or completed transactions.
