**Verified September 21, 2026.** Scope: Rule 4:10 motion seeking a defined examination on good cause; not a unilateral command to attend.

Read [shared decisions](common.md) alongside this file.

## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Rule 4:10(a)–(c)](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) requires a condition in controversy, good cause, notice, defined examination terms, qualified examiner and report arrangements.

## Conditional decisions

- An opposing lawyer cannot compel an examination by letter alone; this command produces a motion, not a self-executing demand.
- Any out-of-state examiner, recording/observer condition or invasive test needs case-specific support and agreement/order as applicable.

## Presentation choices

Court caption with closing parentheses, double spacing, native editable numbering and a counsel/service block. Black 12-point Times New Roman, one-inch margins and page numbers are house choices; this does not establish local-court approval. Keep the final document's legal purpose distinct from the command's legacy label.

## Required case inputs

Condition/pleadings, good-cause evidence, examiner qualifications/location, exact procedures and notice/report dates.
