## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Rule 4:10(a)–(c)](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) governs in-controversy conditions, good cause, specified examination terms and examiner reports.

## Conditional decisions

- Distinguish a request from an entered order. Do not promise an absolute right to a recording, observer or refusal.
- Out-of-state examiners and report-related privilege consequences require the rule-specific analysis. This is procedural advice, not medical advice.
