## Requirements and primary authorities

- [§ 8.01-413.1(B), (E)](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title8.01/chapter14/section8.01-413.1/) identifies covered retained records, timing, reasonable charges and the specified protected-record exception.

## Conditional decisions

- A request for completion of a new wage form is voluntary; statutory access to retained records does not require creating missing records.
- Protected-record handling under subsection E and a later subpoena process require separate steps.
