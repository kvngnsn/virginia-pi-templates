## Requirements and source-supported constraints

Rule 5A:6 controls a separate notice of appeal, including transcript statement, party/counsel information, opposing-counsel transmission, appellate copy and fee. Required cost security is separate from a suspension bond.

## Conditional decisions and boundaries

For an appellee’s assignments of cross-error within an existing appeal, Rules 5A:21 and 5A:25 may be the correct path without a separate notice. This template is only for counsel’s deliberate separate-appeal filing. Do not assume a later deadline merely because another party appealed.
