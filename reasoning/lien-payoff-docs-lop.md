## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Virginia RPC 1.15](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx): addresses protected funds and third-party interests.
- [Virginia Code § 8.01-66.2](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-66.2/): a statutory provider lien must be distinguished from an independently asserted contractual undertaking.

## Conditional decisions

- Read the signed letter of protection and who actually undertook each obligation. Do not infer firm guaranty from a client provider debt.
- Separate a release of proceeds/lien rights from discharge of the patient account and release of counsel’s actual undertaking.

## Presentation choices

Single-spaced block letter with paragraph spacing and a compact signature. No court caption or ornamental rules. These are presentation choices, not a prescribed form.

## Required case inputs

Signed LOP, parties/capacity, payoff approval, services, payment, exact releases and remaining balances.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from requirements, not copied from a commercial form. No facts, signatures, service, consent, notarization, attachments, receipt of money or judicial acts may be invented.
