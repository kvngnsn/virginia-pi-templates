## Requirements and primary authorities

- [§ 8.01-379.2:1](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title8.01/chapter13/section8.01-379.2:1/) governs preservation; [Rules 4:9 and 4:9A](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) govern later formal production where applicable.

## Conditional decisions

- Use a defensible time window, actual camera location and known retention risk.
- Original metadata and clock offsets can matter; do not insist an unknown custodian uses a particular system.

## Presentation choices

Black 12-point Times New Roman, one-inch margins and single spacing. Block letter format with paragraph gaps.

## Required case inputs

Event/time zone, location/views, time window, custodian, retention risk and delivery method.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from the cited requirements; no commercial sample copied. Do not invent consent, service, signatures, facts, attachments, dates or completed transactions.
