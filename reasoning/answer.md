## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Rule 3:8(a)](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) requires an answer responding to complaint paragraphs and forbids a general denial of the entire complaint; its current response periods depend on actual service/waiver.
- [Rules 1:4 and 3:18(c)–(e)](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) address responsive pleading and defenses; [Rule 3:8(a1)](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) restricts serial responsive pleadings after the initial response.

## Conditional decisions

- Use the complete operative complaint; do not produce a fictional response set from a short summary.
- Identify waiver, service date and prior motions; a late defense or later demurrer may require leave.
- Counterclaims and cross-claims need their own supported allegations and relief, not a label in a defense list.

## Presentation choices

Borderless parenthesis caption, black 12-point Times New Roman, one-inch margins, double spacing, restrained headings and page numbers; see common.md. These choices do not establish any local-court approval.

## Required case inputs

Complete operative complaint, defendant’s factual instructions, service/waiver and responsive-pleading history, supported defenses and jury election.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from the cited requirements; no commercial sample copied. Do not invent consent, service, signatures, facts, attachments, dates or completed transactions.
