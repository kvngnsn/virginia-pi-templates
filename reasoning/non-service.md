## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Virginia Code § 8.01-293](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-293/): defines who may serve process.
- [Virginia Code § 8.01-296](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-296/): individual service methods have distinct requirements; entities require their own applicable statute.
- [Virginia Code § 8.01-325](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-325/): governs return, private-server affidavit/annotation and the 72-hour return rule with weekend/holiday treatment.
- [Virginia Code § 8.01-326](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-326/): addresses evidentiary effect and amendment of proof.

## Conditional decisions

- Do not turn attempted service into completed service. Return deadlines, method and recipient facts must be checked.
- A private-server affidavit is signed by the actual server and genuinely sworn; attorney service of later papers is different.

## Presentation choices

Double-spaced court profile with the established borderless parenthesis caption, modest headings and editable numbering. Signature and service blocks are kept together. Caption geometry, Times New Roman 12 and one-inch margins are house choices; the court-paper spacing requirement is distinguished in common.md.

## Required case inputs

Process, recipient, server qualifications, exact attempts or service facts, required mailing, return date and actual notarization.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from requirements, not copied from a commercial form. No facts, signatures, service, consent, notarization, attachments, receipt of money or judicial acts may be invented.
