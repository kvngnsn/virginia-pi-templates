## Requirements and source-supported constraints

TRICARE may pursue federal medical-care recovery; the regional contractor requests DD Form 2527 when third-party liability is implicated. The official instructions require its timely signed return.

## Conditional decisions and boundaries

Use the actual recovery office, not a guessed service branch or mailing address. The cover letter is not a substitute for DD Form 2527 or proof of representative authority.
