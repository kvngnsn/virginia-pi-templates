# Motion for Protective Order

Template: [protective.md](../templates/discovery/protective.md) · Word: [protective.docx](../templates/discovery/docx/protective.docx) · Shared choices: [common.md](common.md)

Verified: September 21, 2026.

**Scope:** Virginia Circuit Court civil personal-injury litigation.

## Requirements and authorities

- [Rule 4:1(c)](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf): good cause, appropriate protection/court and genuine conferral certification.
- [Rule 4:1(b)(6), (7)](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf): separate privilege and inaccessible-ESI procedures where applicable.

## Conditional rules and case decisions

- Filing this motion is not represented as automatically staying every discovery obligation. Request and obtain any needed interim relief.
- A discovery confidentiality order does not automatically seal court filings. Do not make an unsupported blanket privilege or confidentiality designation.

## Presentation decisions

The grounds identify an actual burden or harm and the proposed relief addresses it. Separate sections are an editorial choice.

See [shared formatting decisions](common.md): court requirements, observed caption convention and house style are separate.

## Required case inputs

Actual discovery, supporting burden/harm evidence, proper court, conferral record, alternatives, requested protection and any imminent date.

## Use boundary

An editable drafting starting point. No facts, signature, completed service, consent, judicial ruling or attachment is established by a placeholder.
