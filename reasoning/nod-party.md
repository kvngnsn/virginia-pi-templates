## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Rules 4:5(a), (b)(1), (b)(5), (b)(7), 4:3 and 4:9](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) govern leave where needed, reasonable notice, officer, remote arrangements and party production.

## Conditional decisions

- This is an individual-party notice, not the organizational designation route under Rule 4:5(b)(6) or a nonparty subpoena.
- Avoid assuming a short notice accelerates a document response or permits a remote officer anywhere without authority.
