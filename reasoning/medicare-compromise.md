## Requirements and source-supported constraints

CMS distinguishes beneficiary waiver grounds from discretionary compromise. A waiver requires lack of fault plus hardship or another unfairness basis; compromise has its own written factual showing.

## Conditional decisions and boundaries

Identify which relief is actually sought. Payment and interest obligations and appeal rights remain separate. Do not assume an insurer debtor has beneficiary waiver rights.
