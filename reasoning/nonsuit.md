# Motion for Voluntary Nonsuit

Template: [nonsuit.md](../templates/motions/nonsuit.md) · Word: [nonsuit.docx](../templates/motions/docx/nonsuit.docx) · Shared choices: [common.md](common.md)

Verified: September 21, 2026.

**Scope:** Virginia Circuit Court civil personal-injury litigation.

## Requirements and authorities

- [§ 8.01-380(A)–(E)](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title8.01/chapter13/section8.01-380/): stage restrictions, prior nonsuits, late-notice costs, related claims and tolling cross-reference.
- [Rules 4:15, 1:12, 1:13](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf): notice, service and separate proposed-order presentation.

## Conditional rules and case decisions

- Do not automatically call this a first nonsuit or promise an unconditional six-month refiling period. Refiling/jurisdiction/limitations need case-specific analysis.
- Disclosure of previous nonsuits is required. Related claims may require consent or independent adjudication. The court order is separate and no judge’s signature is manufactured.

## Presentation decisions

Explicit claim/party scope and procedural facts. This is not a federal Rule 41 dismissal notice.

See [shared formatting decisions](common.md): court requirements, observed caption convention and house style are separate.

## Required case inputs

Procedural stage, claims/parties, all previous nonsuits, related claims, consent, trial/notice dates and proposed order.

## Use boundary

An editable drafting starting point. No facts, signature, completed service, consent, judicial ruling or attachment is established by a placeholder.
