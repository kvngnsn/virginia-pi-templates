## Requirements and source-supported constraints

Rules 5A:19(b)(1) and 5A:25 require preliminary assignments even when the electronically filed record makes an appendix unnecessary, subject to the rule’s videotape/order exceptions.

## Conditional decisions and boundaries

The legacy designation-of-record label is corrected to the actual appendix/preliminary-assignment procedure. Verify record filing, agreement, appellant/appellee role and required contents. This is not the transmitted record or assembled appendix.

## Presentation choices

Black Times New Roman, 14 points, Letter paper, one-inch margins and double-spaced body follow Rule 5A:4. A compact caption and centered title are presentation choices; no unnecessary brief front matter is added.

## Required case inputs

Resolve each bracketed field against actual case documents, authorizations, orders and recipient instructions. Do not invent dates, fees, payment, consent, signatures, service, attachments or agency action. Retain unknowns for attorney completion. Any authority or record citation inserted during drafting must be verified for that case.
