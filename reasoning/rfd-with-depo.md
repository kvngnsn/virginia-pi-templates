**Verified September 21, 2026.** Scope: Rule 4:9 production request accompanying a party deposition notice; not a shortened nonparty subpoena.

Read [shared decisions](common.md) alongside this file.

## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Rule 4:5(b)(5)](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) applies Rule 4:9 procedure to accompanying party-deponent requests. [Rules 4:9(b) and 4:1(b)(6)](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) govern particulars, responses, objections and privilege descriptions.

## Conditional decisions

- Calculate the actual response period and scheduling-order deadline; the deposition date alone cannot override them.
- Nonparty production, financial records and health records need their separate protections and procedure.

## Presentation choices

Borderless parenthesis caption, black 12-point Times New Roman, one-inch margins, double spacing, restrained headings and page numbers; see common.md. These choices do not establish any local-court approval.

## Required case inputs

Party status, notice, actual service/deadlines/order, bounded requests, ESI format, reasonable production logistics and any extension.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from the cited requirements; no commercial sample copied. Do not invent consent, service, signatures, facts, attachments, dates or completed transactions.
