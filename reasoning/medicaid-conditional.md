## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Virginia Code § 8.01-66.9](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-66.9/): defines the Commonwealth/program lien, priority and judicial reduction after good-faith compromise efforts.
- [Virginia Code § 8.01-66.9:2](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-66.9:2/): sets a qualified final-demand request and subsequent offer procedure; the initial request requires all relevant claims submitted and processed.
- [Virginia Code § 2.2-514](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/2.2-514/): governs authorized compromise.
- [12VAC30-20-200(7)–(9)](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/admincode/title12/agency30/chapter20/section200/): requires portal submissions and specified information for lien statements, reductions and unrelated-charge disputes.

## Conditional decisions

- This is Virginia Medicaid/DMAS, not Medicare/BCRC or California Medi-Cal. Verify the current designated representative and use the required public-facing portal.
- Representation and HIPAA documentation must satisfy the current regulation, including signatures within the last 12 months. Submit full identifiers and health records securely through the required channel.
- The § 8.01-66.9:2 final-demand clock does not attach to every provisional request. Its subsequent 45-day offer mechanism requires its own predicates and an Attorney General copy.
- Choose one posture and remove the unused branch. Do not assert that pending claims have been processed without evidence.

## Presentation choices

Single-spaced block letter with paragraph spacing and a compact signature. No court caption or ornamental rules. These are presentation choices, not a prescribed form.

## Required case inputs

Current payment status, all claims processing evidence if final, required portal submission, disputed charges and authorized contact.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from requirements, not copied from a commercial form. No facts, signatures, service, consent, notarization, attachments, receipt of money or judicial acts may be invented.
