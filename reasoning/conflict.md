Read [shared decisions](common.md) alongside this file.

## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Rule 1.7(a)–(b)](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx) requires consent after consultation, memorialized in writing, and satisfaction of the competence, legality and same-proceeding limitations. [Rules 1.6, 1.9 and 1.16](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx) may create separate restrictions.

## Conditional decisions

- A signature cannot cure a nonconsentable conflict or disclosure that itself violates confidentiality.
- Do not reuse this as an automatic former-client waiver or blanket future-conflict waiver.
