## Requirements and primary authorities

- [§ 8.01-35.1](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title8.01/chapter3/section8.01-35.1/) affects releases, other tortfeasors and contribution; the scope must identify what is actually settled.
- [RPC 1.2(a), 1.4 and 1.15](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx) govern client authority, explanation and protected funds.

## Conditional decisions

- Check capacity and court approval requirements. The generic text does not supply special statutory signatures/notices under § 38.2-2206(K)–(L).
- Confidentiality, indemnity, unknown-injury language and dismissal triggers are negotiated choices; do not add them automatically.
- Keep payment receipt distinct from promised payment and avoid promising a lienholder’s release without authority.

## Presentation choices

Single-spaced plain document with a descriptive title, short labeled sections and blank execution fields. No court caption or law-firm letter salutation; this is a readability choice, not a prescribed form.

## Required case inputs

Parties/capacity/authority, complete offer and acceptance terms, consideration, precise release, payment timing, liens, costs, reservations, approvals and signatures.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from the cited requirements; no commercial sample copied. Do not invent consent, service, signatures, facts, attachments, dates or completed transactions.
