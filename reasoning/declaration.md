## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Virginia Code § 8.01-4.3](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-4.3/): permits a dated signed declaration with the statutory perjury assurance where an affidavit may be used, subject to specified exceptions.

## Conditional decisions

- Check the particular statute and purpose. Do not replace a deposition, oath of office or an oath required before a specifically designated official other than a notary.
- The declaration is the witness’s factual statement. Counsel must not sign it for the witness.
