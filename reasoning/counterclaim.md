## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Rule 3:9](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) allows a defendant to assert qualifying claims against the plaintiff and supplies timing/response rules; these are not automatically compulsory federal counterclaims.
- [Rules 1:4, 3:18(e) and 3:21](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) govern allegations, combined pleadings and jury demands.

## Conditional decisions

- A new claim needs its own elements, jurisdictional analysis and relief; do not simply relabel an answer.
- Verify the filing clock, any tolling response motion and extension order; preserve service and limitations issues.
