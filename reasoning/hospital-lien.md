## Requirements and primary authorities

- [§ 8.01-66.2](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title8.01/chapter3/section8.01-66.2/) specifies provider categories and lien limits; [§ 8.01-66.5](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title8.01/chapter3/section8.01-66.5/) addresses notice and exceptions.
- [§ 8.01-66.9](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title8.01/chapter3/section8.01-66.9/) separately governs specified Commonwealth/program/public-institution interests; its current text includes 2026 amendments.

## Conditional decisions

- Do not apply the private hospital cap to a Commonwealth program claim.
- The notice exception for known Commonwealth services/payment must be considered; lack of a copied notice does not automatically defeat every lien.
- A lien amount and underlying patient debt may differ.
