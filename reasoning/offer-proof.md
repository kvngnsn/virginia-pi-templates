## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Rule 2:103(a)(2) and (b)](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) requires the substance of excluded evidence to be made known by proffer and protects the jury from inadmissible material.
- [Rules 5:25 and 5A:18](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) govern specific preservation.

## Conditional decisions

- Describe actual evidence, not speculation about what a witness might say.
- Distinguish an offer, an actual exclusion and an admission; obtain a ruling and preserve the proper record.
