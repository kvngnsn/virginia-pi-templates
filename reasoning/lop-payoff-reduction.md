## Requirements and primary authorities

- [RPC 1.15](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx) addresses protected funds; [RPC 4.1](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx) requires truthful negotiation. The actual undertaking and an effective modification control contractual duties.

## Conditional decisions

- A request cannot unilaterally amend a guarantee or assignment. Obtain agreement from the proper holder and obligated persons.
- Do not bundle government or health-plan reimbursement claims into a private LOP reduction.

## Presentation choices

Black 12-point Times New Roman, one-inch margins and single spacing. Block letter format with paragraph gaps.

## Required case inputs

Original undertaking, parties/capacities, verified balance, supported reasons, authorized offer, exact discharge scope and payment terms.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from the cited requirements; no commercial sample copied. Do not invent consent, service, signatures, facts, attachments, dates or completed transactions.
