## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Averett v. Shircliff, 218 Va. 202, 206–07 (1977)](https://law.justia.com/cases/virginia/supreme-court/1977/760750-1.html) addresses repair cost and depreciation within the vehicle-damage measure; the decision text is a primary judicial source reproduced by Justia.

## Conditional decisions

- This is a tort demand; first-party policy coverage requires separate policy analysis.
- Do not add pre-repair market loss, full repair costs and residual diminution as overlapping recoveries. No automatic percentage.
