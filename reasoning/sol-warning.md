## Requirements and primary authorities

- [RPC 1.1, 1.3, 1.4 and 1.16](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx) govern competent deadline advice and protection of the client’s interests.
- [§ 8.01-243](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title8.01/chapter4/section8.01-243/) is a starting point for ordinary personal-injury limitations, not a complete accrual, tolling or notice analysis.

## Conditional decisions

- Counsel must verify claim category, accrual, tolling, service and any government notice requirements before filling the date.
- Do not use a client acknowledgment as a malpractice waiver or as a substitute for protecting an existing client.
