# Virginia complaint reasoning

Template: [complaint.md](../templates/pleadings/complaint.md) · Word: [complaint.docx](../templates/pleadings/docx/complaint.docx) · Shared choices: [common.md](common.md)

This is an original, two-page template built from legal requirements and deliberate formatting choices. It covers an ordinary motor-vehicle negligence claim in Virginia Circuit Court, with one adult individual plaintiff and one individual defendant represented by Virginia counsel.

Bracketed fields remain open. The jury demand is included as an optional election. Additional parties, special claims and longer facts will require adaptation and a fresh pagination check.

## Court requirements versus presentation choices

Rule links below point to the current official compilation. Page numbers are **PDF viewer pages**, verified September 21, 2026.

| Element | Classification | What the template does and why |
|---|---|---|
| Initial pleading | Required | Uses **COMPLAINT**. [Rule 3:2(a), p. 181](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf#page=181). |
| Caption contents | Required | Names the court and all parties. The rule does not prescribe boxes, parentheses or alignment. [Rule 3:2(b), p. 181](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf#page=181). |
| Allegations | Required | Concise essential facts in numbered paragraphs. Numbering is editable Word numbering. [Rule 1:4(d), (j), pp. 9–10](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf#page=9). |
| Damages demand | Required for this monetary claim | Leaves a specific dollar amount for counsel to select. [Rule 3:2(c)(ii), p. 181](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf#page=181). |
| Paper and spacing | Required within the paper-filing rule's scope | Letter paper and double-spaced typed material, including caption and signature. The rule excepts quotations and is subject to electronic-filing provisions. [Rule 1:16(a)(1), p. 25](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf#page=25). |
| Counsel details and signature | Required | Blank signature line, attorney name, VSB number, address, phone, email and conditional fax. [Rules 1:4(c), (l), 1:5(a), pp. 9–11](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf#page=9); [§ 8.01-271.1](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title8.01/chapter7/section8.01-271.1/). |
| Defendant identification | Required information; placement chosen | Allegation 2 supplies the last-known address. The statute requires furnishing the clerk identifying information, not a caption address box. [§ 8.01-290](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title8.01/chapter8/section8.01-290/). |
| Negligence and venue | Case-dependent legal requirements | Prompts for supported duty, breach, causation, injury and venue facts. [Kellermann, slip pp. 7–8](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/opinions/opnscvwp/1081718.pdf#page=7); [§ 8.01-262](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title8.01/chapter5/section8.01-262/). Preferred venue and the actual duty must be checked for the case. |
| Party names left, case number right, parentheses between | Observed convention, selected design | An official appendix illustrates this circuit-court caption arrangement. It is evidence of a convention, not a complaint-specific mandate or a claim that it is the most common. [Part Five Appendix Form 1, p. 445](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf#page=445). |
| Font and margins | Our design choice | Black 12-point Times New Roman, one-inch margins. These values are not prescribed by Rule 1:16. |
| Headings and page numbers | Our design choice | Centered bold title, two restrained section headings, bottom-center page numbers, no visible caption borders. |

## Conditional content

- **Jury:** included as an optional election; remove if not requested or unavailable. [Rule 3:21, p. 204](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf#page=204).
- **Fees:** no automatic attorney-fee request; identify a legal basis first. [Rule 3:25(b), p. 209](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf#page=209).
- **Verification and service:** no generic notary block or claim that initial service already occurred. Verification depends on the governing statute; Rule 1:12 addresses papers after initial process. [Rules 1:10 and 1:12, pp. 19–21](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf#page=19).
- **Local filing:** confirm the selected court's directions and electronic-filing requirements. No locality has been selected for this template. [Rules 1:15–1:17, pp. 24–26](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf#page=24).

## Source and implementation notes

Gaps in the reviewed rules material were filled from current official sources and the [court's amendment index](https://www.vacourts.gov/courts/scv/amend). No sample complaint supplied the wording. The official appendix was used only to evaluate the caption layout.

The template does not assume a rear-end collision, permanent injury, lost wages, punitive damages or particular traffic violations. Facts and the damages amount must come from case material or counsel. A general negligence allegation need not list every particular; the supported breach placeholder is a drafting choice. [Rule 3:18(b), p. 200](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf#page=200).

**Verification:** both rendered pages visually checked; editable numbering, caption columns, font, spacing, paper size and margins checked in the Word file.
