## Requirements and primary authorities

- [RPC 1.15(b)(5)](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx) concerns client/third-party funds and valid liens or assignments; the actual LOP defines any additional undertaking.

## Conditional decisions

- Read the original undertaking and actual signer/capacity. Do not label every treatment bill a LOP.
- Confirm whether satisfaction covers the account, the lien or both, and distinguish an offer from an accepted payoff.

## Presentation choices

Black 12-point Times New Roman, one-inch margins and single spacing. Block letter format with paragraph gaps.

## Required case inputs

Actual LOP, signatories, account, itemization/credits, payoff good-through date, remaining claims, settlement-fund status and secure contact.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from the cited requirements; no commercial sample copied. Do not invent consent, service, signatures, facts, attachments, dates or completed transactions.
