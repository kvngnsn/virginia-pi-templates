**Verified September 21, 2026.** Scope: Case-specific Virginia Circuit Court brief addressing defined trial issues.

## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Rules 1:18, 4:13 and 4:15](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) place scheduling and motion practice within the operative order/procedure.
- [Rule 3.3](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx) requires candor concerning law and facts.

## Conditional decisions

- No universal trial-brief deadline is inserted; use the entered order and local practice.
- Do not represent expected proof as evidence already admitted or rely on the brief alone to preserve an objection.

## Presentation choices

Court caption with closing parentheses, double spacing, native editable numbering and a counsel/service block. Black 12-point Times New Roman, one-inch margins and page numbers are house choices; this does not establish local-court approval. Keep the final document's legal purpose distinct from the command's legacy label.

## Required case inputs

Remaining issues, trial/order dates, stipulated and disputed record, controlling law and exact requested rulings.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted for this purpose; no commercial template copied. Signatures, service, consent, attachments, transactions and facts must reflect actual events. Confirm current law and the entered case orders before use.
