**Verified September 21, 2026.** Scope: Deposition of an identified testifying expert; not discovery of a non-testifying consultant without the required showing.

Read [shared decisions](common.md) alongside this file.

## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Rule 4:1(b)(4)(A)(ii), (C)](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) permits deposition of an identified trial expert and addresses reasonable fees/expenses; subsections (A)(iv)–(vi) protect specified drafts/communications.
- [Rule 4:5](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) controls notice, place, testimony and applicable process.

## Conditional decisions

- Confirm expert status, disclosure and court deadlines. Treating-provider and non-testifying-consultant issues require classification.
- A notice alone does not compel a nonparty or authorize unrestricted expert-file discovery.

## Presentation choices

Borderless parenthesis caption, black 12-point Times New Roman, one-inch margins, double spacing, restrained headings and page numbers; see common.md. These choices do not establish any local-court approval.

## Required case inputs

Actual expert disclosure, subjects, scheduling order, party/nonparty status, date/place, officer, method, fees and any proper subpoena.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from the cited requirements; no commercial sample copied. Do not invent consent, service, signatures, facts, attachments, dates or completed transactions.
