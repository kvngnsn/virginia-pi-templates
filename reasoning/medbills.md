# Request for Itemized Medical Bills

Template: [medbills.md](../templates/medical/medbills.md) · Word: [medbills.docx](../templates/medical/docx/medbills.docx) · Shared choices: [common.md](common.md)

Verified: September 21, 2026.

**Scope:** Virginia personal-injury matter; ordinary adult claimant.

## Requirements and authorities

- The no-charge entitlement is limited to the account balance or itemized listing and the statutory request frequency. [§ 8.01-413(B5)](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title8.01/chapter14/section8.01-413/).

## Conditional rules and case decisions

- Do not describe charges as amounts paid, balances as liens, or adjustments as patient payments. Additional ledger detail is requested only if maintained.
- Confirm authorization and request history. An authorization is a separate document.

## Presentation decisions

Short letter with a precise records request; no pleading caption, notary or payment undertaking.

See [shared formatting decisions](common.md): court requirements, observed caption convention and house style are separate.

## Required case inputs

Patient, provider, service dates, account if known, authorization, request history and delivery destination.

## Use boundary

An editable drafting starting point. No facts, signature, completed service, consent, judicial ruling or attachment is established by a placeholder.
