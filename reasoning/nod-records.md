## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Rule 4:9A(a)–(f)](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) governs issuance, party copies, production, objections and special health records; it was amended effective July 1, 2025.
- [§ 32.1-127.1:03](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/32.1-127.1:03/) supplies separate protected-health-record procedures referenced by Rule 4:9A(e).

## Conditional decisions

- The legacy command says records-only deposition; the Virginia artifact is a notice accompanying nonparty records process. It cannot replace an issued subpoena.
- Health records, protected officials, out-of-state custodians and short compliance periods require their own analysis and documents.
