## Requirements and primary authorities

- [45 C.F.R. § 164.508](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/subtitle-A/subchapter-C/part-164/subpart-E/section-164.508) governs authorization-based disclosure; this letter does not itself supply patient authorization.
- [Rule 1.6](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx) requires protection of client information.

## Conditional decisions

- Use a valid signed authorization and secure identifiers. Do not import patient-access deadlines or fee caps into every attorney authorization request.
- Paid amounts, adjustments and patient responsibility must remain separate to prevent double counting.

## Presentation choices

Block letter with single spacing and paragraph gaps; no court caption. Black 12-point Times New Roman, one-inch margins and page numbers are house choices; this does not establish local-court approval. Keep the final document's legal purpose distinct from the command's legacy label.

## Required case inputs

Plan/custodian, signed authorization, service dates, member reference, payment categories and delivery approval.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted for this purpose; no commercial template copied. Signatures, service, consent, attachments, transactions and facts must reflect actual events. Confirm current law and the entered case orders before use.
