## Requirements and source-supported constraints

Rule 5A:33 addresses panel rehearing after final disposition: 14 days, longer of 25 pages or 5,300 words, no response unless requested, and no oral argument. Rule 5A:3 permits discretionary extensions on good cause; filing a motion does not toll time. Rule 5A:4 requires a compliant cover and certification.

## Conditional decisions and boundaries

This is not en banc rehearing under 5A:34 or rehearing of a petition denial under 5A:15. The optional contents/authorities leaves are a design choice. Do not carry brief-specific word-count exclusions into this petition without checking the applicable rule.

## Presentation choices

Black Times New Roman, at least 14 points, Letter paper, one-inch margins and double-spaced body. Those appellate typography requirements come from Rule 5A:4. Centered cover, optional contents/authorities leaves and native tab stops are presentation choices; final PDF bookmarks, labels and contents references require case-specific preparation.

## Required case inputs

Resolve each bracketed field against actual case documents, authorizations, orders and recipient instructions. Do not invent dates, fees, payment, consent, signatures, service, attachments or agency action. Retain unknowns for attorney completion. Any authority or record citation inserted during drafting must be verified for that case.
