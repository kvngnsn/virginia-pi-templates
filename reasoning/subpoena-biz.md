# Business Records Subpoena Duces Tecum reasoning

**Verified September 21, 2026.** Scope: Attorney-issued Virginia Rule 4:9A nonparty business-records discovery; excludes medical records, trial-only process and specially protected officials.

Read [shared decisions](common.md) alongside this file.

## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Rule 4:9A](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf): defines issuance, same-day clerk copy, notice to parties, production terms and objection/protection procedures.
- [Virginia Code § 8.01-407](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-407/): limits attorney issuance and identifies excluded proceedings.
- [Virginia Code § 8.01-420.9](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-420.9/): protects covered nonparty financial and privileged records.

## Conditional decisions

- Only an active Virginia Bar attorney may issue this version; signature and service are not generated as accomplished facts.
- Health records require the separate § 8.01-413 / § 32.1-127.1:03 process. Rule 4:9A(d) officials and trial production under (e) require separate treatment.
- There is no imported federal universal 14-day objection deadline. The less-than-14-day rule concerns the time allowed for compliance.
- Use the actual served subpoena and return. Do not treat the counsel certificate as proof of service on the nonparty.
