## Decision and boundary

Virginia bank-account enforcement uses the applicable garnishment process and prerequisites, not a generic bank levy letter. Direct Circuit Court users to the existing /garnishment-suggestion and /garnishment workflows; obtain actual issued and served process and include required exemption material. Other courts require their appropriate forms. Existing PDF/filler bytes remain unchanged and their deferred review is not marked complete.
