## Requirements and source-supported constraints

Virginia Circuit civil form inventory distinguishes CC-1433 waiver request, CC-1434 waiver and CC-1406 acceptance. Existing native form workflows remain unchanged.

## Conditional decisions and boundaries

A receipt acknowledgment is not automatically a waiver or acceptance of service. The correct court, authorized recipient and requested legal effect must be selected first.
