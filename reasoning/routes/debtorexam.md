# Judgment debtor interrogatory summons

Route entry: `/debtorexam` in [ROUTES.md](../../templates/ROUTES.md). No drafting template is provided for this entry.

## Decision and boundary

Section 8.01-506 supplies the judgment-debtor interrogatory process and authorizes issuance by the clerk or qualifying commissioner. The Circuit Court publisher identifies CC-1481; /summons-interrogatories remains available and unchanged. The new route explains the actual issuing actor; it does not create an issued summons or complete the deferred form/filler review.
