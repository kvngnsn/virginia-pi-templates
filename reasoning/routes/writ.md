## Decision and boundary

Sections 8.01-466 and 8.01-474 govern issuance of execution and its form/direction. Request an applicable writ from the clerk after checking enforceability and any stay. CC-1418 is service on the Secretary of the Commonwealth, not execution. A generic lawyer-drafted writ does not supply issuance.
