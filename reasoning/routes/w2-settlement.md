## Requirements and source-supported constraints

IRS settlement guidance identifies taxable wage treatment when the recovery replaces taxable wages, subject to the claim’s actual nature and applicable exclusions. W-2 instructions govern official wage reporting.

## Conditional decisions and boundaries

No payroll calculation, withholding, transmission or filing is performed. Do not convert qualifying physical-injury recovery to wages merely because lost earnings were a damages measure.
