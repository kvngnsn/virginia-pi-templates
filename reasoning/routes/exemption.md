# Garnishment or lien exemption claim

Route entry: `/exemption` in [ROUTES.md](../../templates/ROUTES.md). No drafting template is provided for this entry.

Reviewed September 21, 2026. Command `/exemption`. Outcome: `OFFICIAL_PROCESS`.

## Decision and boundary

Use current DC-454, July 2026, with the front notice and reverse hearing request. Section 8.01-512.4 governs the notice/claim procedure. Identify the actual court, parties and claimed exemption; submit the signed claim to its clerk. This is a publisher-form handoff, not automated PDF filling, a new Word motion or a determination that a particular asset is exempt.
