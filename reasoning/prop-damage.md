## Requirements and primary authorities

- [§ 8.01-379.2:1](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-379.2:1/) applies where inspection/repair affects evidence in foreseeable litigation.
- [Rules 1.2 and 1.4](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx) govern client authority and communication.

## Conditional decisions

- Confirm ownership and actual inspection authority; an estimate does not authorize repairs.
- Address storage mitigation without ordering destruction of evidence.

## Presentation choices

Block letter with single spacing and paragraph gaps; no court caption. Black 12-point Times New Roman, one-inch margins and page numbers are house choices; this does not establish local-court approval. Keep the final document's legal purpose distinct from the command's legacy label.

## Required case inputs

Vehicle/owner, location, damage scope, inspection consent, preservation hold and charge information.

## Scope and verification limits
