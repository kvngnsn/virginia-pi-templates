## Requirements and primary authorities

- [§ 8.01-66.2](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title8.01/chapter3/section8.01-66.2/) addresses specified provider liens; [§ 8.01-66.5](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title8.01/chapter3/section8.01-66.5/) addresses written notice.
- [RPC 1.15](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx) governs protected funds. A contract or assignment may create an issue distinct from the statutory lien.

## Conditional decisions

- Identify whether the claim is a statutory lien, contract, assignment or ordinary debt. Do not treat a lien cap as a discharge of every balance.
- Government and plan claims follow distinct regimes, including § 8.01-66.9 for specified Commonwealth interests.
