## Requirements and source-supported constraints

§ 8.01-424(D)(3) permits payment to a duly qualified fiduciary after inquiry into bond adequacy. This proposed order adds case-specific bank restrictions. Other statutory distribution routes, including §§ 8.01-424(D)(1), (D)(4), (E) and 8.01-606, are distinct.

## Conditional decisions and boundaries

Circuit Court only; use solely the qualified-fiduciary route after approval of the compromise. A bank account by itself is not a statutory substitute for qualification, bond inquiry or the approval order. Institution acceptance and restrictions require case review; this is not a universally mandated Virginia blocked-account form.
