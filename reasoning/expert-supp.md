## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Rule 4:1(e)(1), (4)](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) requires prompt supplementation of specified expert information and permits signed written updates; [Rule 4:1(b)(4)(A)(i)](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) identifies disclosure content.

## Conditional decisions

- Do not invent a rebuttal opinion or relabel a late new opinion as timely supplementation.
- The actual scheduling order controls deadlines and required leave; supplementation does not automatically reopen discovery.

## Presentation choices

Borderless parenthesis caption, black 12-point Times New Roman, one-inch margins, double spacing, restrained headings and page numbers; see common.md. These choices do not establish any local-court approval.

## Required case inputs

Prior disclosure/request, expert’s actual new information/opinions, date learned, grounds/materials, order/deadlines and any leave.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from the cited requirements; no commercial sample copied. Do not invent consent, service, signatures, facts, attachments, dates or completed transactions.
