## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Rule 4:10(c)(3)](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) applies subdivision (c) to agreed examinations unless the agreement expressly provides otherwise; subsection (c)(2) addresses report use and a deposition-triggered privilege consequence.

## Conditional decisions

- State actual agreed exceptions to report procedure explicitly; do not promise all privileges remain untouched regardless of later acts.
- A disputed demand is not a stipulation. Obtain each party signature and any required order.

## Required case inputs
