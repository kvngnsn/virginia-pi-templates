## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Rules 1.2, 1.4 and 4.1](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx) require client authority, communication and truthfulness.
- [§ 8.01-243](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-243/) is one potential limitations source; correspondence alone does not toll a claim.

## Conditional decisions

- Separate coverage denial from fault/causation denial; identify the carrier and actual claimant relationship.
- No invented statutory appeal period, automatic bad-faith finding or tolling agreement.

## Presentation choices
