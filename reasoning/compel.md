# Motion to Compel Discovery

Template: [compel.md](../templates/discovery/compel.md) · Word: [compel.docx](../templates/discovery/docx/compel.docx) · Shared choices: [common.md](common.md)

Verified: September 21, 2026.

**Scope:** Virginia Circuit Court civil personal-injury litigation.

## Requirements and authorities

- [Rule 4:12(a)(1)–(4)](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf): appropriate court, permissible motion, genuine conferral certification and conditional expenses; evasive/incomplete answers may be treated as failures.
- [Rule 4:8(c)(2)](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf): provide the relevant discovery items and certificates when disputed.

## Conditional rules and case decisions

- Do not assume 21 days elapsed, that two letters were sent, or that no responses exist. Compute from actual service and extensions.
- Fees and sanctions are not automatic boilerplate. RFA sufficiency uses Rule 4:11(a); adapt the motion to its actual procedural basis. Notice/briefing must follow Rule 4:15 and local procedure.

## Presentation decisions

Compact facts, issue-specific grounds, precise relief and separate factual conferral certification. Parenthesis caption follows the approved complaint.

See [shared formatting decisions](common.md): court requirements, observed caption convention and house style are separate.

## Required case inputs

Actual requests/responses, service proof, due-date computation, order history, conferral evidence, precise cure, exhibits and hearing arrangements.

## Use boundary

An editable drafting starting point. No facts, signature, completed service, consent, judicial ruling or attachment is established by a placeholder.
