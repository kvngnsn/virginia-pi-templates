# Itemized Closing Statement reasoning

**Verified September 21, 2026.** Scope: Virginia client fee/expense and settlement statement; distinct from a court bill of taxable costs.

Read [shared decisions](common.md) alongside this file.

## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Virginia RPC 1.5(c)](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx): requires the contingent-fee conclusion statement showing outcome and remittance calculation.
- [Virginia RPC 1.15](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx): requires trust accounting, records and proper handling of disputed funds.

## Conditional decisions

- Use the actual signed fee agreement for percentage, base and cost deduction order; no standard percentage is inserted.
- No charge or lien is created by the statement. Reconcile all figures and do not count an outstanding reserve as an already-paid expense.

## Presentation choices

Single-spaced plain document with short labeled sections, readable execution fields and page numbers. No pleading caption. This is a house design, not a court-mandated form.

## Required case inputs

Fee agreement, full ledger, actual receipts/clearance, itemized expenses, credits, lien approvals, reserves, prior distributions and arithmetic.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from requirements, not copied from a commercial form. No facts, signatures, service, consent, notarization, attachments, receipt of money or judicial acts may be invented.
