## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Rules 2:401–2:403](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) address relevance and exclusion balancing where applicable; other grounds need their own rule and foundation.
- [Rules 4:15 and 1:18; Part One Appendix Form 3, section VII](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) govern motion practice and scheduling when the order applies.

## Conditional decisions

- Do not assume every adverse fact is unfairly prejudicial or that a label creates a categorical exclusion.
- Use the actual evidence and opposing purpose. Preserve objections at trial as the ruling and law require.
- A request needing more than five minutes of argument has pretrial-setting significance under the uniform order, if that order governs.
