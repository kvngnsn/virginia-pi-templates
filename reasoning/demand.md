# Settlement Demand

Template: [demand.md](../templates/demand/demand.md) · Word: [demand.docx](../templates/demand/docx/demand.docx) · Shared choices: [common.md](common.md)

Verified: September 21, 2026.

**Scope:** Virginia personal-injury matter; ordinary adult claimant.

## Requirements and authorities

- The client controls settlement decisions. [Virginia RPC 1.2(a)](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx).
- Compromise-evidence restrictions are conditional; a heading does not create blanket confidentiality or privilege. [Rule 2:408](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf).

## Conditional rules and case decisions

- No generic statutory response deadline, mandatory release, waiver of other coverage or automatic bad-faith consequence is inserted.
- Every amount, attachment, future-care assertion, permanent impairment and wage-loss allegation needs source support and actual client authority.

## Presentation decisions

Liability, injuries/damages and resolution headings; no COMES NOW, court caption or boilerplate citation dump. The headings are editorial choices.

See [shared formatting decisions](common.md): court requirements, observed caption convention and house style are separate.

## Required case inputs

Liability evidence, injury record, accurately categorized losses, client-approved demand/terms, insured/claim, attachments and proposed response date.

## Use boundary

An editable drafting starting point. No facts, signature, completed service, consent, judicial ruling or attachment is established by a placeholder.
