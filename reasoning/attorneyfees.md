**Verified September 21, 2026.** Scope: Virginia civil fee application supported by an actual contractual, statutory or other recognized fee basis.

Read [shared decisions](common.md) alongside this file.

## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Rule 3:25](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf): requires a pleaded fee demand and basis subject to its exclusions, with pretrial procedure on motion.
- [Virginia RPC 1.5](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx): addresses reasonableness and fee communications; it does not itself create fee shifting.

## Conditional decisions

- Virginia does not award all prevailing-party attorney fees automatically. Identify a real fee-shifting source.
- A new posttrial motion cannot by itself cure waiver of an unpleaded demand; evaluate amendment and jurisdiction.
