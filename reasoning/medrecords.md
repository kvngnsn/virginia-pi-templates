# Request for Medical Records

Template: [medrecords.md](../templates/medical/medrecords.md) · Word: [medrecords.docx](../templates/medical/docx/medrecords.docx) · Shared choices: [common.md](common.md)

Verified: September 21, 2026.

**Scope:** Virginia adult patient records requested by the patient’s authorized attorney for anticipated or pending litigation.

## Requirements and authorities

- The attorney request requires the patient-signed writing and compliance with state privacy provisions. [§ 8.01-413(B), (B1), (B8)](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title8.01/chapter14/section8.01-413/); [§ 32.1-127.1:03(E), (G)](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title32.1/chapter5/section32.1-127.1:03/).

## Conditional rules and case decisions

- The 30-day initial period has a written-delay procedure. Do not collapse it into an unconditional deadline or assume patient-access fee rules apply to all attorney requests.
- Sensitive records, minors, personal representatives and federally restricted records require separate authority checks. This template does not create the authorization.

## Presentation decisions

Block letter. Patient identifiers stay in the recipient-specific letter, not a public court caption. Categories and dates are selected rather than requesting unrelated lifetime records.

See [shared formatting decisions](common.md): court requirements, observed caption convention and house style are separate.

## Required case inputs

Patient identity, treatment range, provider, actual signed authorization, authorized recipient, secure delivery method and approved costs.

## Use boundary

An editable drafting starting point. No facts, signature, completed service, consent, judicial ruling or attachment is established by a placeholder.
