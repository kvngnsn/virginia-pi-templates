# Proposed Verdict Form reasoning

Template: [verdictform.md](../templates/trial/verdictform.md) · Word: [verdictform.docx](../templates/trial/docx/verdictform.docx) · Shared choices: [common.md](common.md)

**Verified September 21, 2026.** Scope: Counsel submission and separate general verdict leaf for a simple single-plaintiff single-defendant damages claim.

Read [shared decisions](common.md) alongside this file.

## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Rule 3:21](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) preserves the applicable jury-trial right; the verdict must fit the issues properly submitted.
- [Rule 1:18 and Form 3 X](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) provide the instruction/tender context; no universal statewide mandatory generic verdict layout is claimed.

## Conditional decisions

- This is a deliberately limited general verdict; do not force multi-party or multi-claim litigation into one plaintiff/defendant choice.
- The jury leaf requires court approval, case-specific final text and foreperson execution only after deliberation.

## Presentation choices

Court caption with closing parentheses, double spacing, native editable numbering and a counsel/service block. Black 12-point Times New Roman, one-inch margins and page numbers are house choices; this does not establish local-court approval. Keep the final document's legal purpose distinct from the command's legacy label.

## Required case inputs

Single claim/parties, approved instructions, supported damages categories and any need for a different verdict structure.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted for this purpose; no commercial template copied. Signatures, service, consent, attachments, transactions and facts must reflect actual events. Confirm current law and the entered case orders before use.
