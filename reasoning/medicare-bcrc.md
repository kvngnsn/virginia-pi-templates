## Requirements and source-supported constraints

BCRC requires adequate proof of representation to act with counsel. Consent to release information grants a narrower role and is not interchangeable.

## Conditional decisions and boundaries

Verify a signed, dated authorization and the person entitled to act for an estate; this letter alone is not proof of representation.
