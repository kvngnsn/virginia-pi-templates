## Requirements and source-supported constraints

26 USC § 104(a)(2) distinguishes qualifying personal physical injury/sickness damages and exceptions. Wages, punitive damages, interest and emotional-distress payments cannot be classified by label alone. Applicable reporting and withholding duties remain the payer’s.

## Conditional decisions and boundaries

Use only supported, genuinely agreed allocations. Do not invent a tax-advantaged split, a tax indemnity or a representation that the IRS accepts the allocation. Court approval and tax counsel review depend on the actual case.
