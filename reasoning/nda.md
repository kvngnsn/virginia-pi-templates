# Confidentiality Agreement reasoning

Template: [nda.md](../templates/settlement/nda.md) · Word: [nda.docx](../templates/settlement/docx/nda.docx) · Shared choices: [common.md](common.md)

**Verified September 21, 2026.** Scope: Negotiated private Virginia civil disclosure agreement; not a court protective order, employment NDA or public-agency secrecy promise.

Read [shared decisions](common.md) alongside this file.

## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Rules 4:1(c) and 3:26](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf): court protection and injunctive relief require their own legal showing.
- [Virginia Code § 2.2-514](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/2.2-514/): restricts confidentiality in covered Commonwealth settlements.
- [Virginia RPC 1.6, 3.4 and 5.6](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx): professional confidentiality, access to evidence and practice restrictions cannot be overwritten by boilerplate.

## Conditional decisions

- The core contract terms are negotiated choices, not court rules. No automatic mutuality, indemnity, liquidated damages or fee shifting is supplied.
- Do not use this for regulated employment, sexual-harassment or government settlements without separate statutory review.

## Presentation choices

Single-spaced plain document with short labeled sections, readable execution fields and page numbers. No pleading caption. This is a house design, not a court-mandated form.

## Required case inputs

Actual disclosures, purpose, permitted recipients, duration, retention, enforcement terms, exclusions and authority.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from requirements, not copied from a commercial form. No facts, signatures, service, consent, notarization, attachments, receipt of money or judicial acts may be invented.
