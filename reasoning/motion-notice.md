# Notice of Motion Hearing

Template: [motion-notice.md](../templates/motions/motion-notice.md) · Word: [motion-notice.docx](../templates/motions/docx/motion-notice.docx) · Shared choices: [common.md](common.md)

Verified: September 21, 2026.

**Scope:** Virginia Circuit Court civil motion notice; use the locality’s required praecipe instead where applicable.

## Requirements and authorities

- [Rule 4:15(a), (b), (c), (e)](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf): proper setting; reasonable notice; seven-day baseline absent exceptions; differing brief schedules; conferral certification and actual-receipt service rule.
- [Rule 1:15(b)](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf): where a court uses a praecipe system, its praecipe substantially follows the Part One appendix.

## Conditional rules and case decisions

- A local praecipe or required scheduling form must be used where applicable. This generic notice does not itself reserve a hearing.
- A supporting brief of five or fewer pages ordinarily changes the notice/filing lead time to 14 days absent leave; do not apply a universal seven-day deadline.

## Presentation decisions

A short hearing logistics block with a separate certification. The format is a notice, not an imitation of a particular county’s mandatory form.

See [shared formatting decisions](common.md): court requirements, observed caption convention and house style are separate.

## Required case inputs

Confirmed setting, proper local procedure, motion, service/filing dates, brief length/schedule, conferral and actual recipients.

## Use boundary

An editable drafting starting point. No facts, signature, completed service, consent, judicial ruling or attachment is established by a placeholder.
