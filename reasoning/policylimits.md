## Requirements and primary authorities

- [RPC 1.2(a), 1.4 and 4.1](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx) require actual client authority, communication and truthful claim assertions.
- [§ 8.01-35.1](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title8.01/chapter3/section8.01-35.1/) addresses release effects; [§ 38.2-2206(K)–(M)](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title38.2/chapter22/section38.2-2206/) contains special UIM settlement, signature, notice and litigation provisions where applicable.

## Conditional decisions

- Use verified policy limits; /limits is the separate disclosure request. Do not manufacture an insurer bad-faith deadline or automatically threaten an excess judgment.
- A demand alone is not the final signed K/L settlement agreement; required notice and initials cannot be supplied by generic release language.

## Presentation choices

Black 12-point Times New Roman, one-inch margins and single spacing. Block letter format with paragraph gaps.

## Required case inputs

Actual client authority, limits evidence, liability/damages support, enclosures, named released parties, complete proposed terms and justified response date.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from the cited requirements; no commercial sample copied. Do not invent consent, service, signatures, facts, attachments, dates or completed transactions.
