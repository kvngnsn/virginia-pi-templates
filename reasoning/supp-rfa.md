## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Rule 4:11(a), (e)](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) distinguishes response obligations, separately stated matters, document copies and the cumulative thirty-request limit excluding genuineness requests.
- [§ 8.01-420(A)](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-420/) permits properly framed underlying-fact admissions derived from depositions, not requests referring to the deposition or asking whether testimony occurred.

## Conditional decisions

- Check all prior sets and parts/subparts; genuineness is not an unlimited vehicle for substantive admissions.
- No federal thirty-day deadline or automatic restart of the cap.

## Presentation choices

Court caption with closing parentheses, double spacing, native editable numbering and a counsel/service block. Black 12-point Times New Roman, one-inch margins and page numbers are house choices; this does not establish local-court approval. Keep the final document's legal purpose distinct from the command's legacy label.

## Required case inputs

Prior counts, agreements/leave, exact facts, document copies/locations and service dates.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted for this purpose; no commercial template copied. Signatures, service, consent, attachments, transactions and facts must reflect actual events. Confirm current law and the entered case orders before use.
