# Proposed Supplemental Juror Questionnaire reasoning

**Verified September 21, 2026.** Scope: Case-specific Virginia civil supplemental questionnaire for court approval; not the clerk’s statutory qualification form.

Read [shared decisions](common.md) alongside this file.

## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Virginia Code § 8.01-358](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-358/): provides examination of prospective jurors for qualification, interests, relationship, opinion and bias.
- [Rules 1:15 and 4:13](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf): local and pretrial procedures affect submission and use.

## Conditional decisions

- The statute does not create an automatic right to distribute counsel’s questionnaire. Obtain approval of content, timing and access.
- Avoid identifying insurance, case persuasion, intrusive irrelevant questions or promises to decide a particular way. Confidentiality or sealing requires lawful court action, not this label.

## Presentation choices

Double-spaced court profile with the established borderless parenthesis caption, modest headings and editable numbering. Signature and service blocks are kept together. Caption geometry, Times New Roman 12 and one-inch margins are house choices; the court-paper spacing requirement is distinguished in common.md.

## Required case inputs

Court approval, neutral issue description, party/witness list, juror-number method and handling instructions.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from requirements, not copied from a commercial form. No facts, signatures, service, consent, notarization, attachments, receipt of money or judicial acts may be invented.
