## Requirements and primary authorities

- [§ 8.01-66.2](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-66.2/), [§ 8.01-66.5](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-66.5/) and [§ 8.01-66.9](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-66.9/) distinguish private provider and Commonwealth claims.
- [Rule 1.15](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx) governs actual disbursements and accounting.

## Conditional decisions

- Verify the actual creditor and signed compromise; no presumed statutory-lien priority or automatic remaining-debt cancellation.
- Do not describe a planned wire as completed.
