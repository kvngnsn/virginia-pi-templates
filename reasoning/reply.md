# Reply Memorandum in Support of Motion reasoning

Template: [reply.md](../templates/motions/reply.md) · Word: [reply.docx](../templates/motions/docx/reply.docx) · Shared choices: [common.md](common.md)

**Verified September 21, 2026.** Scope: Reply on an existing Virginia Circuit Court motion when the applicable schedule or permission allows it.

Read [shared decisions](common.md) alongside this file.

## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Rule 4:15(c)](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) establishes general motion-brief practice but does not supply a universal automatic reply timetable; actual orders and local practice matter.

## Conditional decisions

- Do not assert an entitlement to a reply or surprise the other side with new relief.
- Use the actual opposition, not a fabricated adversary argument.

## Presentation choices

Borderless parenthesis caption, black 12-point Times New Roman, one-inch margins, double spacing, restrained headings and page numbers; see common.md. These choices do not establish any local-court approval.

## Required case inputs

Motion, opposition, permission/schedule, precise contested points, verified record and authorities.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from the cited requirements; no commercial sample copied. Do not invent consent, service, signatures, facts, attachments, dates or completed transactions.
