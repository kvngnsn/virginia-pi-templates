**Verified September 21, 2026.** Scope: Virginia Circuit Court pretrial statement responding to an actual scheduling direction; not an automatic California-style joint pretrial order.

Read [shared decisions](common.md) alongside this file.

## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Rules 1:18 and 4:13](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) govern scheduling and pretrial proceedings; Part One Appendix Form 3 is an ordinary uniform scheduling-order reference.

## Conditional decisions

- Use the entered case order and local requirements. Do not import the eminent-domain Form 3-A deadlines or claim statewide use of an identical statement.
- A joint filing requires actual agreement/authority; include only verified stipulations and identify disputed positions accurately.

## Presentation choices

Borderless parenthesis caption, black 12-point Times New Roman, one-inch margins, double spacing, restrained headings and page numbers; see common.md. These choices do not establish any local-court approval.

## Required case inputs

Entered scheduling order, trial details, remaining issues, actual agreements, witness/exhibit materials, pending motions and court-specific requirements.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from the cited requirements; no commercial sample copied. Do not invent consent, service, signatures, facts, attachments, dates or completed transactions.
