## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Rules 1:18 and 4:13; Part One Appendix Form 3, section V](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) address pretrial lists and exchanges when applicable; the actual order controls.

## Conditional decisions

- Distinguish exhibit list filing from filing the exhibits themselves.
- Record stipulations only when made; listing a document does not cure hearsay, authentication or other evidentiary defects.

## Presentation choices

Borderless parenthesis caption, black 12-point Times New Roman, one-inch margins, double spacing, restrained headings and page numbers; see common.md. These choices do not establish any local-court approval.

## Required case inputs

Actual order, exhibit inventory and identifiers, prior production, required copies, sponsoring basis, stipulations and objections.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from the cited requirements; no commercial sample copied. Do not invent consent, service, signatures, facts, attachments, dates or completed transactions.
