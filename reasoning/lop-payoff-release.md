## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Rule 1.15](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx) governs release of held funds and accounting.
- [§ 8.01-66.2](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-66.2/) and [§ 8.01-66.5](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-66.5/) require distinguishing statutory liens from contract undertakings.

## Conditional decisions

- A payoff quote is not a signed satisfaction; specify effective payment conditions.
- Explicitly resolve whether the patient balance is discharged; a lien release alone is not a general release of debt.
