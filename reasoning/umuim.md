## Requirements and primary authorities

- [§ 38.2-2206](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title38.2/chapter22/section38.2-2206/) governs Virginia UM/UIM coverage and includes policy/election, process and settlement/subrogation provisions.

## Conditional decisions

- Applicable policy date and elections matter; do not automatically offset liability limits or assume a universal consent-to-settle procedure.
- Unknown-driver cases and uninsured named-driver cases have different proof and procedural issues.

## Presentation choices

Black 12-point Times New Roman, one-inch margins and single spacing. Block letter format with paragraph gaps.

## Required case inputs

Policy and effective dates, insured status, adverse coverage evidence, incident, suit/service status, policy elections and actual deadlines.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from the cited requirements; no commercial sample copied. Do not invent consent, service, signatures, facts, attachments, dates or completed transactions.
