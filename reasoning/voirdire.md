## Requirements and primary authorities

- [§ 8.01-358](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-358/) permits relevant inquiry into relationship, interest, prior opinion and bias, and provides for disclosure of knowledge of facts in issue.

## Conditional decisions

- Adapt to actual parties/issues without trying the case during selection.
- Do not inject insurance, settlement or other inadmissible matter through a generic question absent a supported basis and court permission.
