## Requirements and primary authorities

- [§ 8.01-243](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-243/) is a limitations starting point, not a universal deadline for every PI-related claim.
- [Rules 1.3, 1.4 and 1.16](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx) require diligence, communication and protection on termination; [Rule 1:5](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) governs withdrawal of counsel of record.

## Conditional decisions

- Calculate from actual accrual, tolling, party/capacity and prior filings; no default two-year insertion.
- An urgent letter cannot substitute for leave to withdraw or erase an existing duty.
