## Requirements and source-supported constraints

Rule 5A:28 governs ordinary argument and special requests, including amicus participation. Brief certificates separately state oral-argument waiver elections.

## Conditional decisions and boundaries

Not a routine prerequisite to every appeal: draft only for a concrete request beyond the ordinary process. No oral argument is available on a Rule 5A:33 rehearing petition.

## Presentation choices

Black Times New Roman, 14 points, Letter paper, one-inch margins and double-spaced body follow Rule 5A:4. A compact caption and centered title are presentation choices; no unnecessary brief front matter is added.

The blank draft starts its closing substantive paragraph on a new page to keep it with the signature and service block. This is a presentation choice; reflow the completed document before filing.
