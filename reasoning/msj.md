## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Rule 3:20](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) requires the parties to be at issue and no genuine dispute of material fact.
- [§ 8.01-420](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-420/) restricts discovery-deposition use and contains particular punitive-damages/business-entity exceptions; it also addresses properly framed admissions.

## Conditional decisions

- Do not import federal Rule 56 affidavit/deposition practice. An ordinary individual PI case is not the all-business-entity exception.
- An exception concerning punitive damages does not automatically authorize summary judgment on compensatory liability.

## Presentation choices

## Required case inputs

Operative pleadings, permitted admissions/orders, exact requested issue, disputed facts and any valid evidentiary exception.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted for this purpose; no commercial template copied. Signatures, service, consent, attachments, transactions and facts must reflect actual events. Confirm current law and the entered case orders before use.
