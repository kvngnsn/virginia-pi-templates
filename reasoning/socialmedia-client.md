## Requirements and primary authorities

- [§ 8.01-379.2:1](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title8.01/chapter13/section8.01-379.2:1/) ties preservation duties to relevant evidence and reasonably foreseeable litigation.
- [RPC 3.4(a), 1.1 and 1.6](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx) address evidence integrity, competent assistance and confidentiality.

## Conditional decisions

- Advice against deletion covers unfavorable evidence too. Do not instruct a client to scrub an account.
- Collection, privacy changes and account access need a case-specific preservation plan; no subpoena or automatic remote access is created.

## Required case inputs

Incident, reasonable dates/topics, known accounts and devices, automatic-deletion risks, secure collection method and contact person.
