# Plaintiff’s First Interrogatories to Defendant

Template: [interrogatories.md](../templates/discovery/interrogatories.md) · Word: [interrogatories.docx](../templates/discovery/docx/interrogatories.docx) · Shared choices: [common.md](common.md)

Verified: September 21, 2026.

**Scope:** Plaintiff to individual defendant in ordinary Virginia Circuit Court motor-vehicle litigation; tailor direction and relevance.

## Requirements and authorities

- [Rule 4:8(a), (b), (d), (g)](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf): availability, restatement, oath, specific objections and cumulative 30-interrogatory limit including parts/subparts.
- [Rule 4:1(b)](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf): relevant nonprivileged scope; insurance agreements have a distinct provision.

## Conditional rules and case decisions

- Count prior sets and every subpart before serving. The eight starter questions do not establish the remaining limit.
- The ordinary 21-day response rule has a 28-day defendant provision and court modifications; compute from actual service. Serve rather than routinely file under Rule 4:8(c).

## Presentation decisions

Focused motor-vehicle starter questions, restrained heading and continuous numbering. Discovery is double-spaced as house style even when not filed.

See [shared formatting decisions](common.md): court requirements, observed caption convention and house style are separate.

## Required case inputs

Correct parties/roles, incident definition, prior discovery count, service and court-order dates; tailor/remove questions before use.

## Use boundary

An editable drafting starting point. No facts, signature, completed service, consent, judicial ruling or attachment is established by a placeholder.
