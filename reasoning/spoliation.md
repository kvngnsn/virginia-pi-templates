## Requirements and primary authorities

- [§ 8.01-379.2:1](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-379.2:1/) ties the duty to reasonably foreseeable litigation and calibrates remedies to prejudice and, for severe remedies, recklessness or intent. It creates no independent spoliation claim.
- [Rules 3.4(a) and 4.2](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx) address preservation and communications with represented persons.

## Conditional decisions

- Distinguish a real follow-up concern from a generic initial hold notice. Do not label conduct intentional without evidence.
- The requested response date is a proposal, not a statutory deadline.

## Presentation choices
