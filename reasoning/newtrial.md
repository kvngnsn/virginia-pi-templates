## Requirements and primary authorities

- [§ 8.01-383](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-383/) authorizes civil-jury new trials and limits repeated new trials on the contrary-to-evidence ground.
- [§ 8.01-430](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-430/) may require final judgment rather than another trial where the record permits decision.
- [Rule 1:1](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) supplies the ordinary jurisdictional control period.

## Conditional decisions

- Do not conflate a new-trial request with JNOV or assume every error warrants retrial.
- Address prejudice, preservation, remedy and actual jurisdiction; after-discovered evidence requires its own supported standard.

## Presentation choices

Court caption with closing parentheses, double spacing, native editable numbering and a counsel/service block. Black 12-point Times New Roman, one-inch margins and page numbers are house choices; this does not establish local-court approval. Keep the final document's legal purpose distinct from the command's legacy label.

## Required case inputs

Verdict/judgment, transcript and objections, specific prejudice, prior new trials, jurisdiction dates and exact relief.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted for this purpose; no commercial template copied. Signatures, service, consent, attachments, transactions and facts must reflect actual events. Confirm current law and the entered case orders before use.
