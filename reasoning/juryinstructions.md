**Verified September 21, 2026.** Scope: Counsel submission and repeatable separate instruction leaf; all substantive instruction text must be case-selected.

Read [shared decisions](common.md) alongside this file.

## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Rule 1:18 and Part One Appendix Form 3 X](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) address exchange and tender of instructions; the actual entered order governs.
- [Virginia Model Jury Instructions — Civil](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/circuit/resources/model_jury_instructions_civil.pdf) provides subject-specific forms to evaluate, not automatic proof that every instruction fits the evidence.
- [Rules 5:25 and 5A:18](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) require preservation of specific objections.

## Conditional decisions

- Use one instruction per leaf and separate authorities/arguments from jury text.
- This original submission framework does not preselect substantive instructions or reproduce a commercial annotated set. Court acceptance must be recorded only after ruling.

## Presentation choices

Court caption with closing parentheses, double spacing, native editable numbering and a counsel/service block. Black 12-point Times New Roman, one-inch margins and page numbers are house choices; this does not establish local-court approval. Keep the final document's legal purpose distinct from the command's legacy label.

## Required case inputs

Actual claims/evidence, selected instruction text/authority/version, changes, order dates and opposing position.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted for this purpose; no commercial template copied. Signatures, service, consent, attachments, transactions and facts must reflect actual events. Confirm current law and the entered case orders before use.
