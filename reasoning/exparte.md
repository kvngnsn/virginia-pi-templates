## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Rule 3:26](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf): requires adequate notice ordinarily, a specific sworn emergency showing and written notice certification for no-notice relief; preliminary-injunction standards are separately stated.
- [Virginia Code § 8.01-624](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-624/): requires stated duration.
- [Virginia Code § 8.01-631](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-631/): addresses security before relief takes effect.

## Conditional decisions

- Emergency labeling does not authorize undisclosed contact or eliminate notice. Select the appropriate noticed or no-notice branch.
- A preliminary injunction requires the separate Rule 3:26(c)–(e) showing; this short-term TRO template does not substitute for that application.

## Required case inputs

Sworn facts, immediate timing, underlying claim, notice efforts, precise restraint, hearing and bond.

Original wording drafted from requirements, not copied from a commercial form. No facts, signatures, service, consent, notarization, attachments, receipt of money or judicial acts may be invented.
