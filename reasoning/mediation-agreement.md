## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Virginia Code § 8.01-581.22](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-581.22/): defines private mediation confidentiality and exceptions.
- [Virginia Code § 8.01-576.9](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-576.9/): court-referred dispute resolution has a separate confidentiality framework.
- [Virginia RPC 1.2 and 1.4](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx): client authority and communication remain necessary.

## Conditional decisions

- Private and court-referred mediation statutes must be matched to the actual process.
- Mediation confidentiality is not blanket privilege for every source document or final agreement. No automatic settlement, waiver or release is inserted.

## Presentation choices

Single-spaced plain document with short labeled sections, readable execution fields and page numbers. No pleading caption. This is a house design, not a court-mandated form.

## Required case inputs

Parties, authority, mediator, conflicts, referral status, schedule, fees, cancellation and confidentiality choices.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from requirements, not copied from a commercial form. No facts, signatures, service, consent, notarization, attachments, receipt of money or judicial acts may be invented.
