## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Rules 1:13 and 1:1](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) govern presentation and entered-order consequences. [§ 8.01-35.1](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title8.01/chapter3/section8.01-35.1/) makes the intended effect on other persons/claims significant.

## Conditional decisions

- Use /nonsuit for that distinct procedure. Do not automatically substitute federal Rule 41 terminology.
- Check settlement performance and all cross-claims before saying a case is concluded; select prejudice terms deliberately.

## Presentation choices

Borderless parenthesis caption, black 12-point Times New Roman, one-inch margins, double spacing, restrained headings and page numbers; see common.md. These choices do not establish any local-court approval.

## Required case inputs

Actual settlement and performance, exact parties/claims, dismissal terms, costs, remaining claims and required endorsements.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from the cited requirements; no commercial sample copied. Do not invent consent, service, signatures, facts, attachments, dates or completed transactions.
