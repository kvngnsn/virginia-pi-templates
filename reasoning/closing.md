**Verified September 21, 2026.** Scope: Closing an actually completed adult PI engagement; not terminating ongoing litigation without court leave.

Read [shared decisions](common.md) alongside this file.

## Requirements and primary authorities

- [RPC 1.16(d)–(e)](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx) addresses client interests and file/property return; [RPC 1.15 and 1.5(c)](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx) address property accounting and contingent-matter closing statements.

## Conditional decisions

- Do not state representation ended in active court litigation unless required leave/withdrawal steps are satisfied.
- Do not release unresolved trust funds or invent lien clearance. File retention policy is not universal permission to destroy originals.

## Presentation choices

Black 12-point Times New Roman, one-inch margins and single spacing. Block letter format with paragraph gaps.

## Required case inputs

Actual resolution, scope completion, payment/accounting status, remaining work, deadlines, client materials and established retention policy.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from the cited requirements; no commercial sample copied. Do not invent consent, service, signatures, facts, attachments, dates or completed transactions.
