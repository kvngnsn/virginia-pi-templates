## Requirements and primary authorities

- [RPC 1.4(a)–(c)](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx) requires reasonable updates, explanations sufficient for informed decisions and communication of material settlement developments.

## Conditional decisions

- Separate confirmed dates from estimates and cite the actual order or notice in working records.
- Do not manufacture progress, a pending offer or a client decision.

## Presentation choices

Black 12-point Times New Roman, one-inch margins and single spacing. Block letter format with paragraph gaps.

## Required case inputs

As-of date, verified recent events, open issues, tasks, actual deadlines and offers, and responsible contacts.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from the cited requirements; no commercial sample copied. Do not invent consent, service, signatures, facts, attachments, dates or completed transactions.
