## Requirements and primary authorities

- [RPC 1.5(a)–(c), (g)](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx) requires reasonableness, adequate fee explanation, a written contingent-fee method including rates/expense treatment, a closing statement and no nonrefundable advanced legal fee.
- [RPC 1.2, 1.8(e), 1.15 and 1.16](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx) govern client decisions, litigation-expense assistance, trust property and termination.

## Conditional decisions

- All percentages, stages, cost allocation and termination terms must be selected and explained; no default market percentage is invented.
- Rule 1.5(c) requires writing; client/firm signatures are a deliberate execution safeguard, not an assertion that its text expressly mandates both signatures.
- Check special statutory fee limits and outside-counsel division rules when applicable. No malpractice waiver, assignment of the claim or automatic settlement power is included.

## Presentation choices

Single-spaced plain document with a descriptive title, short labeled sections and blank execution fields. No court caption or law-firm letter salutation; this is a readability choice, not a prescribed form.

## Required case inputs

Client capacity, exact scope, rates by stage, expense treatment, sample arithmetic using actual terms, appeal scope, termination terms and actual signatories.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from the cited requirements; no commercial sample copied. Do not invent consent, service, signatures, facts, attachments, dates or completed transactions.
