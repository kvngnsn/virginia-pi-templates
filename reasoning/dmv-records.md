## Requirements and primary authorities

- [§ 46.2-208](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/46.2-208/) has multiple effective-date versions. Use the version effective on the request date; attorney status alone does not confer unrestricted driver-history access.
- [DMV records guidance](https://www.dmv.virginia.gov/records/request-driver-vehicle-record) and [CRD-93](https://www.dmv.virginia.gov/sites/default/files/forms/crd93.pdf) identify submission and identity requirements.

## Conditional decisions

- Crash vehicle-owner information under subsection B(17) differs from unrestricted driver information.
- This original letter supplements, rather than rewrites, the current official form.

## Presentation choices

Block letter with single spacing and paragraph gaps; no court caption. Black 12-point Times New Roman, one-inch margins and page numbers are house choices; this does not establish local-court approval. Keep the final document's legal purpose distinct from the command's legacy label.

## Required case inputs

Record type, subject identifiers, client authority, current form, fee and secure delivery method.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted for this purpose; no commercial template copied. Signatures, service, consent, attachments, transactions and facts must reflect actual events. Confirm current law and the entered case orders before use.
