## Requirements and primary authorities

- [§ 8.01-581.22](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title8.01/chapter21.2/section8.01-581.22/) protects specified mediation communications with exceptions; pre-existing discoverable material does not become immune merely by use in mediation.
- [Rule 2:408](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) and [RPC 1.2, 1.4 and 1.6](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx) address settlement evidence, authority and confidential information.

## Conditional decisions

- A signed mediated agreement is not confidential under § 8.01-581.22 unless the parties otherwise agree in writing.
- Follow the actual mediator’s submission and exchange requirements; do not file a mediator-only narrative as a public brief.

## Presentation choices

Single-spaced plain document with a descriptive title, short labeled sections and blank execution fields. No court caption or law-firm letter salutation; this is a readability choice, not a prescribed form.

## Required case inputs

Actual submission rules, audience, case/evidence summary, damages, authorized negotiation history, risks, participants and exhibits.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from the cited requirements; no commercial sample copied. Do not invent consent, service, signatures, facts, attachments, dates or completed transactions.
