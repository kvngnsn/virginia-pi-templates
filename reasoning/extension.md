# Motion to Extend Time to File Responsive Pleading

Template: [extension.md](../templates/motions/extension.md) · Word: [extension.docx](../templates/motions/docx/extension.docx) · Shared choices: [common.md](common.md)

Verified: September 21, 2026.

**Scope:** Virginia Circuit Court extension of responsive-pleading time under Rule 1:9; not an appeal or venue deadline.

## Requirements and authorities

- [Rule 1:9](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf): extension of pleading time lies with the court and may be granted after expiration, with a specific venue-challenge exception.
- [Rules 4:15, 1:12](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf): presentation, notice and service.

## Conditional rules and case decisions

- This is a motion, not a unilateral notice that extends a deadline. Agreement alone does not establish court entry.
- Exclude venue challenges and other non-pleading deadlines unless separately researched. If default or judgment exists, an extension alone may not supply the required relief.

## Presentation decisions

Short numbered procedural facts and precise requested date. The generic catalog’s “notice” label is overridden for this VA template.

See [shared formatting decisions](common.md): court requirements, observed caption convention and house style are separate.

## Required case inputs

Service, correct existing deadline, requested date, reasons, prior extensions, default status, other-party position and conferral.

## Use boundary

An editable drafting starting point. No facts, signature, completed service, consent, judicial ruling or attachment is established by a placeholder.
