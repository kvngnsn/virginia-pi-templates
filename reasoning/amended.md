## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Rule 1:8](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) requires leave to amend and ordinarily a properly executed proposed amended pleading with a written motion; the grant order controls filing.
- [Rules 3:2, 1:4 and 3:18](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) govern caption, facts, numbered allegations and monetary relief. [Rule 3:21](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) controls jury demand timing.

## Conditional decisions

- Do not assert amendment as of right or treat opposing counsel’s consent as court leave.
- Check new-party limitations/relation-back issues separately; amendment does not itself resolve them.
- After a sustained demurrer, preserving the original pleading under § 8.01-273(B) has specific requirements; do not add a blanket preservation statement without checking the order.

## Presentation choices

Borderless parenthesis caption, black 12-point Times New Roman, one-inch margins, double spacing, restrained headings and page numbers; see common.md. These choices do not establish any local-court approval.

## Required case inputs

Operative pleading, leave order, complete party facts, supported claim and damages, amendments sought, service needs and actual jury-demand history.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from the cited requirements; no commercial sample copied. Do not invent consent, service, signatures, facts, attachments, dates or completed transactions.
