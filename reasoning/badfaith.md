## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Virginia Code § 8.01-66.1](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-66.1/): contains distinct motor-vehicle and UM/UIM remedies with different prerequisites; subsections D–F include a 45-day notice structure.
- [Virginia Code § 38.2-209](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/38.2-209/): addresses insured-versus-insurer fee recovery upon the specified bad-faith finding.

## Conditional decisions

- Default scope is an ordinary third-party settlement-opportunity letter, not a § 8.01-66.1(D) UIM notice. Generate that notice only after verifying its own conditions.
- Do not threaten automatic treble damages, an invented deadline, assignment of the insured’s rights or entitlement to policy limits without an actual basis.

## Presentation choices

Single-spaced block letter with paragraph spacing and a compact signature. No court caption or ornamental rules. These are presentation choices, not a prescribed form.

## Required case inputs

Policy/claim/insured, demand terms, liability and damages proof, deadline, coverage posture and actual standing.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from requirements, not copied from a commercial form. No facts, signatures, service, consent, notarization, attachments, receipt of money or judicial acts may be invented.
