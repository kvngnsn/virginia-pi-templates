## Requirements and primary authorities

- [29 U.S.C. § 1132(a)(3)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/29/1132): limits the described enforcement route to appropriate equitable relief.
- [US Airways, Inc. v. McCutchen, 569 U.S. 88 (2013)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/11-1285): plan terms control the equitable lien by agreement; equitable defaults do not override clear terms.
- [Montanile, 577 U.S. 136 (2016)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/14-723): addresses tracing and general assets in the specific § 502(a)(3) enforcement setting.
- [Virginia RPC 1.15](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx): governs protected funds and accounting.

## Conditional decisions

- Verify ERISA status, funding, governing terms, claimant authority and the applicable plan year; an insurer’s assertion alone does not establish these.
- Do not assume a common-fund reduction overrides clear plan text. Do not advise dissipation of disputed proceeds; professional trust obligations remain separate.
- Verify payee/payment instructions independently. Do not make a receipt, cleared-payment or release representation from a planned disbursement alone.

## Presentation choices

Single-spaced block letter with paragraph spacing and a compact signature. No court caption or ornamental rules. These are presentation choices, not a prescribed form.

## Required case inputs

Executed payoff terms, amount, verified payee, actual payment status/reference, scope and confirmation recipient.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from requirements, not copied from a commercial form. No facts, signatures, service, consent, notarization, attachments, receipt of money or judicial acts may be invented.
