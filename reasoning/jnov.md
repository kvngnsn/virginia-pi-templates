## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Virginia Code § 8.01-430](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-430/): permits final judgment when the verdict lacks support and the matter can be decided on the complete evidence.
- [Rule 1:1](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf): entry, jurisdiction and valid suspension control timing.

## Conditional decisions

- A timely filing does not itself preserve jurisdiction after 21 days. Obtain any necessary entered suspension order.
- Do not substitute dissatisfaction with the verdict for the statutory and case-law standard.
