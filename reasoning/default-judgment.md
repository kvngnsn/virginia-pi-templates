## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Rule 3:19](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf): governs default, written motion notice, proof of damages and relief.
- [Virginia Code § 8.01-296](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-296/): additional prerequisites apply after posting.
- [Virginia Code § 8.01-15.2](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-15.2/): military status protections.
- [50 U.S.C. § 3931](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/50/3931): requires military-status evidence and additional protections where applicable.

## Conditional decisions

- The defendant-specific mailing under Rule 3:19(c)(1) is separate from ordinary service on counsel. Neither is pre-certified.
- Do not equate an unanswered complaint with automatic entitlement to the requested amount. Verify proof, military status, actual service and the jurisdictional deadline.
