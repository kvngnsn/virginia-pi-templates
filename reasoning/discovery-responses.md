# Responses to Discovery

Template: [discovery-responses.md](../templates/discovery/discovery-responses.md) · Word: [discovery-responses.docx](../templates/discovery/docx/discovery-responses.docx) · Shared choices: [common.md](common.md)

Verified: September 21, 2026.

**Scope:** Modular responses to actual served Virginia Circuit Court interrogatories, RFPs or RFAs; retain only requested modules.

## Requirements and authorities

- [Rule 4:8(b), (d)](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf): reproduce interrogatories; separate sworn answers; party signature and counsel-signed objections.
- [Rules 4:9(b)(ii), 4:11(a)](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf): request-specific responses, withholding disclosure, and actual reasonable inquiry for inability to admit or deny.

## Conditional rules and case decisions

- Select only the applicable response module, repeat it for every actual request and remove unused sections. The displayed one-per-type layout is a pattern, not a three-request response limit.
- Retain the oath only for interrogatory answers; it is not a universal RFP/RFA requirement. Never invent a denial, privilege, production, inquiry or notarization.

## Presentation decisions

Original request followed immediately by its response. Three clearly separated modules; no omnibus boilerplate objections and no complaint allegations.

See [shared formatting decisions](common.md): court requirements, observed caption convention and house style are separate.

## Required case inputs

Full actual served requests, responding party, source-supported answers, actual inquiry/production status, particular objections, service dates and signer.

## Use boundary

An editable drafting starting point. No facts, signature, completed service, consent, judicial ruling or attachment is established by a placeholder.
