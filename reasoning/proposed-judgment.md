## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Virginia Code § 8.01-382](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-382/): distinguishes prejudgment and judgment interest.
- [Virginia Code § 6.2-302](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/6.2-302/): governs the applicable judgment interest rate.
- [Rules 1:1 and 1:13](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf): govern finality, entry and presentation.

## Conditional decisions

- Confirm credits and recoverable interest bases; do not automatically apply six percent where a different lawful rate controls.
- Unresolved fee or claim issues require a specific finality analysis.

## Presentation choices

Double-spaced court profile with the established borderless parenthesis caption, modest headings and editable numbering. Signature and service blocks are kept together. Actual record and rulings, every claim and party, award terms, dates, entry and presentation procedure.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from requirements, not copied from a commercial form. No facts, signatures, service, consent, notarization, attachments, receipt of money or judicial acts may be invented.
