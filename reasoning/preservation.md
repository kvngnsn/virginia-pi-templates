# Preservation of Evidence

Verified: September 21, 2026.

**Scope:** Virginia personal-injury matter; ordinary adult claimant.

## Requirements and authorities

- The preservation duty turns on relevant evidence and reasonably foreseeable litigation, evaluated in context. [§ 8.01-379.2:1(A)](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title8.01/chapter13/section8.01-379.2:1/).

## Conditional rules and case decisions

- The statute does not make sanctions automatic and does not create a standalone spoliation claim. Avoid guaranteed adverse-inference or default-judgment threats.
- The acknowledgment date is a requested business deadline, not a statutory deadline. Identify evidence narrowly enough to be actionable.

## Presentation decisions

Letter format; one focused evidence description instead of a universal demand to retain every corporate record.

See [shared formatting decisions](common.md): court requirements, observed caption convention and house style are separate.

## Required case inputs

Incident, actual foreseeability facts, recipient control, relevant systems/items, retention risks, time range and requested acknowledgment date.

## Use boundary

An editable drafting starting point. No facts, signature, completed service, consent, judicial ruling or attachment is established by a placeholder.
