## Requirements and source-supported constraints

SCV Rules 5:1B, 5:6 and 5:17 govern this civil petition: exact preserved assignments, tables, pertinent proceedings/facts, one-place argument and standards for each error, precise relief, certificate, filing/service and length. Rule 5:17(f) allows the longer of 35 pages or 6,125 words, subject to leave and listed exclusions.

## Conditional decisions and boundaries

Supreme Court of Virginia civil discretionary petition for appeal only. Not a U.S. Supreme Court certiorari petition, Rule 5:17A interlocutory petition, petition for rehearing or criminal Anders filing. Verify appellate jurisdiction and statutory exceptions; do not assume direct appeal from an ordinary circuit civil judgment. Add one argument section per assignment; table pages and counts must be recomputed after drafting.

## Presentation choices

Use the established black Times New Roman and one-inch Letter layout. Supreme Court Rule 5:6 requires at least 14-point approved type, double-spaced body with the specified single-spaced exceptions. A separate cover, Roman-numbered tables and Arabic body numbers organize this reference; exact filing and paper-cover requirements remain court-controlled.

## Required case inputs

Use actual case records, contracts, orders, authorizations and recipient instructions. Do not invent facts, authority, payments, attachments, consent, service, signatures, specialist work or agency action. Bracketed fields remain unresolved until supported. Verify any legal or record citation added while drafting for the specific case.
