## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Rule 4:5(b)(6)](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) requires particularized subjects, organizational designation and good-faith topic conferral; a nonparty subpoena must advise of both duties.
- [Rule 4:5(a1), (b)(1), (5), (7)](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) governs place, notice, associated production and remote arrangements.

## Conditional decisions

- Identify party status and obtain required nonparty process; notice is not a subpoena.
- Do not demand a single omniscient witness or disclose privileged information without a basis.
- Use actual lawful location and leave requirements for early or special depositions.
