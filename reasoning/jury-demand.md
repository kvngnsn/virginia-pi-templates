**Verified September 21, 2026.** Scope: Written Virginia Circuit Court jury demand on issues triable of right; no imported jury-fee-posting procedure.

Read [shared decisions](common.md) alongside this file.

## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Rule 3:21(b)–(d)](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) addresses service and filing, the last pleading directed to the issue, court-set dates, issue specification and waiver/leave. Amendment does not extend the period unless the leave order expressly says so.

## Conditional decisions

- Verify both filing and service, and whether a prior demand already covers the issue.
- Do not copy another state’s automatic jury-fee deposit requirement or treat an unavailable jury issue as a right.

## Presentation choices

Borderless parenthesis caption, black 12-point Times New Roman, one-inch margins, double spacing, restrained headings and page numbers; see common.md. These choices do not establish any local-court approval.

## Required case inputs

Issues, actual pleadings/service history, any court-set demand date, prior demand, leave order and party election.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from the cited requirements; no commercial sample copied. Do not invent consent, service, signatures, facts, attachments, dates or completed transactions.
