# Motion to Continue Trial

Template: [continuance.md](../templates/motions/continuance.md) · Word: [continuance.docx](../templates/motions/docx/continuance.docx) · Shared choices: [common.md](common.md)

Verified: September 21, 2026.

**Scope:** Virginia Circuit Court civil personal-injury litigation.

## Requirements and authorities

- [Rules 1:18, 1:20, 4:15](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf): scheduling orders, trial-setting procedure and motion presentation. The actual scheduling order and local rules must be read.

## Conditional rules and case decisions

- No statewide automatic entitlement or universal filing lead time is asserted. Identify the applicable local standard and order.
- A trial continuance does not silently extend every discovery/expert deadline. Specify each requested change and do not state an order has been granted.

## Presentation decisions

Facts, diligence and concrete scheduling relief; no stock emergency allegation or invented consent.

See [shared formatting decisions](common.md): court requirements, observed caption convention and house style are separate.

## Required case inputs

Actual trial/order dates, reasons and diligence, prior requests, prejudice, participant availability, opposing position and requested changes.

## Use boundary

An editable drafting starting point. No facts, signature, completed service, consent, judicial ruling or attachment is established by a placeholder.
