## Requirements and source-supported constraints

§ 38.2-2209 has policy/insurer/vehicle predicates; it is not a universal bar to every out-of-state PIP or benefits recovery claim.

## Conditional decisions and boundaries

Virginia-matter coverage inquiry and conditional response. The incident state alone does not establish policy law; no unsupported common-fund, made-whole, no-fault or reimbursement rule is asserted. Statutory health-plan liens use their distinct routes.
