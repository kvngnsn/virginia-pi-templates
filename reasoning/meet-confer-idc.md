## Requirements and source-supported constraints

Rules 4:12(a)(2) and 4:15(b) require appropriate good-faith conferral/certification for discovery motions; Rule 4:15 governs motion scheduling and notice. A universal Virginia informal discovery conference procedure was not verified.

## Conditional decisions and boundaries

Use as counsel-to-counsel conferral correspondence. A local judicial conference can be requested only if the actual court offers it; follow that court’s process and avoid ex parte merits contact. This letter does not replace a motion, hearing notice or actual certification.
