## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Rule 1.15](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx) governs safekeeping and disputed funds.
- [§ 8.01-66.2](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-66.2/) and [§ 8.01-66.5](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-66.5/) govern certain provider liens and notice; a contractual undertaking is distinct.

## Conditional decisions

- Do not create a personal guarantee or concede debt/lien validity merely because a provider requests a letter.
- State actual agreed collection terms, scope and balance; do not manufacture a negotiated reduction.

## Presentation choices

Standalone document with single spacing, restrained headings and explicit fields; no court caption. Black 12-point Times New Roman, one-inch margins and page numbers are house choices; this does not establish local-court approval. Keep the final document's legal purpose distinct from the command's legacy label.

## Required case inputs

Provider identity, itemized balance, treatment dates, lien priority, client consent and agreed undertaking.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted for this purpose; no commercial template copied. Signatures, service, consent, attachments, transactions and facts must reflect actual events. Confirm current law and the entered case orders before use.
