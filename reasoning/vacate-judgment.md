## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Rule 1:1](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf): ordinarily limits trial-court control to 21 days after entry.
- [Virginia Code § 8.01-428](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-428/): separates default grounds, clerical correction, lack-of-notice relief and preserved independent actions.
- [Virginia Code § 8.01-623](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-623/): bill of review has its own scope, leave and time requirements.

## Conditional decisions

- This motion cannot create post-21-day jurisdiction. Use only a verified ground and the procedure that ground requires.
- An independent action or bill of review is not automatically accomplished by filing this generic motion; switch to that appropriate pleading if required.
