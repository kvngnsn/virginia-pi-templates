## Requirements and source-supported constraints

A settlement may allocate specified liabilities among its parties but cannot extinguish a nonsignatory’s independent claim. The actual signatory, consideration and scope must be established.

## Conditional decisions and boundaries

Do not impose personal indemnity on counsel or conceal an unlimited undertaking inside a closeout form. Separate contractual terms from mandatory law; no generic indemnity language is represented as court required.
