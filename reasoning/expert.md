# Expert Witness Disclosure

Template: [expert.md](../templates/discovery/expert.md) · Word: [expert.docx](../templates/discovery/docx/expert.docx) · Shared choices: [common.md](common.md)

Verified: September 21, 2026.

**Scope:** Virginia Circuit Court civil personal-injury litigation.

## Requirements and authorities

- [Rule 4:1(b)(4)(A)(i)](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf): identity, subject matter, substance of facts/opinions and summary of grounds in expert discovery.
- [Rules 4:1(e), 1:18](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf): supplementation and actual pretrial scheduling order.

## Conditional rules and case decisions

- Do not import FRCP 26(a)(2) report requirements or deadlines as Virginia state rules. Read the actual discovery request and scheduling order.
- Do not invent medical opinions, credentials, reliance materials or an unlimited right to supplement. Protected drafts and attorney communications are not automatic attachments.

## Presentation decisions

One repeatable expert block, with opinions separated from grounds. CV/report lists are conditional, not obligatory boilerplate.

See [shared formatting decisions](common.md): court requirements, observed caption convention and house style are separate.

## Required case inputs

Expert-approved opinions and grounds, credentials/contact details, actual request/order, deadline and actual attachments.

## Use boundary

An editable drafting starting point. No facts, signature, completed service, consent, judicial ruling or attachment is established by a placeholder.
