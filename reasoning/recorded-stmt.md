## Requirements and primary authorities

- [RPC 1.2, 1.4 and 4.1](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx) require authority, informed client decisions and accuracy.
- [Rule 4:1(b)(3)](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) addresses a person’s own prior statement in discovery; the pre-suit copy request does not claim that all discovery procedures are already available.

## Conditional decisions

- Verify the carrier’s role. First-party, UM/UIM, examination-under-oath and compulsory testimony require different analysis.

## Presentation choices

Black 12-point Times New Roman, one-inch margins and single spacing. Block letter format with paragraph gaps.

## Required case inputs

Actual statement request, carrier role, client decision, policy/compulsory-process status, prior statement details and contact.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from the cited requirements; no commercial sample copied. Do not invent consent, service, signatures, facts, attachments, dates or completed transactions.
