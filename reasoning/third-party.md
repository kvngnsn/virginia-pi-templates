**Verified September 21, 2026.** Scope: Derivative claim against a nonparty who may owe all or part of the defending party’s liability.

Read [shared decisions](common.md) alongside this file.

## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Rule 3:13](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) requires potential derivative liability and leave when filed later than 21 days after service of the original responsive pleading.
- [Rules 3:5, 3:6 and 1:12](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) distinguish initial process on a new party from later paper service.

## Conditional decisions

- Do not use impleader solely to assert that a stranger directly owes the original plaintiff; plead the derivative basis.
- The certificate covers actual later-paper service only. Obtain and complete initial summons/service separately.
