# Motion to Strike the Evidence reasoning

**Verified September 21, 2026.** Scope: Virginia civil trial motion challenging evidentiary sufficiency, not a California pleading motion or federal directed verdict.

Read [shared decisions](common.md) alongside this file.

## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Virginia Code § 8.01-378](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-378/): preserves a motion to strike despite the prohibition on directed verdicts.
- [Rule 1:4](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf): supports particularized motion grounds; trial preservation must be tied to the actual record.

- [Collelo v. Geographic Services, Inc., 283 Va. 56 (2012), official opinion](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/opinions/opnscvwp/1101411.pdf): reviews evidentiary sufficiency in the light most favorable to the nonmoving party. Apply the standard to the actual claim and trial record.

## Conditional decisions

- The ambiguous catalog label is narrowed to the Virginia evidence motion. A motion attacking a pleading requires separate analysis.
- Keep grounds specific, preserve/renew them as the trial record requires, and verify current case authority for the precise claim.
