## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Rule 4:15(c), (e)](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) governs motion briefs, length, timing and actual receipt; the court may set another schedule. [Rule 1:4](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) and [§ 8.01-271.1](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title8.01/chapter7/section8.01-271.1/) require supported signed filings.

## Conditional decisions

- Do not use one standard for demurrer, summary judgment and discovery motions.
- Verify actual hearing/brief length schedule; no universal deadline is calculated from this skeleton.

## Presentation choices

Borderless parenthesis caption, black 12-point Times New Roman, one-inch margins, double spacing, restrained headings and page numbers; see common.md. These choices do not establish any local-court approval.

## Required case inputs

Motion, complete relevant record, issues, burdens, verified authorities, actual briefing order/hearing and exhibits.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from the cited requirements; no commercial sample copied. Do not invent consent, service, signatures, facts, attachments, dates or completed transactions.
