# Pre-Suit Policy Limits Disclosure Request

Template: [limits.md](../templates/insurance/limits.md) · Word: [limits.docx](../templates/insurance/docx/limits.docx) · Shared choices: [common.md](common.md)

Verified: September 21, 2026.

**Scope:** Pre-suit Virginia motor-vehicle personal-injury disclosure under § 8.01-417(C); excludes wrongful-death subsection D.

## Requirements and authorities

- Subsection C sets pre-suit motor-vehicle scope, notice, identifying information, supporting records, the $12,500 or enumerated-charge trigger, and response timing. [§ 8.01-417(C), (E), (F)](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title8.01/chapter14/section8.01-417/).

## Conditional rules and case decisions

- If the trigger or required documents are missing, request voluntary disclosure and do not assert a mandatory limits deadline. The address-disclosure obligation is separately stated in the statute.
- Do not substitute wrongful-death subsection D, a policy-limits settlement demand, or post-filing Rule 4:1(b)(2) discovery. Send to the insurer, not merely its agent.

## Presentation decisions

Letter with an explicit eligibility and enclosure paragraph. No invented two-week deadline or bad-faith threat.

See [shared formatting decisions](common.md): court requirements, observed caption convention and house style are separate.

## Required case inputs

Pre-suit status, representation notice, claimant, insured, accident, insurer, report, medical records/bills, applicable wage loss and verified statutory trigger.

## Use boundary

An editable drafting starting point. No facts, signature, completed service, consent, judicial ruling or attachment is established by a placeholder.
