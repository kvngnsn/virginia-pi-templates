## Requirements and primary authorities

Current [Virginia Rules of Court](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) were downloaded and compared with the existing snapshot; SHA-256 is `15a5095aba5043b863d2c7fb26f24c2556cad4908054874c0dff4f770af53a0f`. The [amendments index](https://www.vacourts.gov/courts/scv/amend) was checked for effective changes.

- Rules 5A:1(c), 5A:4 and 5A:19 govern filing/service, signed certificates, cover information, typography, length and briefing schedule. Rule 5A:4 generally requires at least 14-point approved type, double-spaced text subject to its exceptions and at least one-inch margins on letter paper.
- Rule 5A:20 requires contents/authorities, case statement, a separately headed **Assignments of Error** section, exact preservation references, record-supported facts, each issue's standard and argument in one place, precise relief, counsel identification and service/oral-argument/word-count certification. Rule 5A:18 controls preservation exceptions.
- Rule 5A:19(a) provides the longer of 50 pages or 12,300 words absent permission; the template leaves actual word count and any enlarged-limit order unfilled. The count excludes the permitted matter, not substantive headings, footnotes or quotations.
- The appeal-of-right opening deadline normally runs from record filing; the discretionary-appeal trigger differs. Verify the court notice, current rules and any order rather than pre-populating a due date.
- [Court brief instructions](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/cav/forms/formatting_instructions_briefs.pdf) require a searchable PDF, bookmarks, an unnumbered cover, separately numbered preliminary tables and body numbering. [Publisher opening skeleton](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/cav/forms/opening_brief_template.pdf) confirms the broad structure but its example facts and annotations are omitted.

## Conditional decisions

- Appellant is the appealing party; do not equate appellant with the trial plaintiff. This template is for a civil merits appeal from a circuit court, not a Supreme Court petition, agency appeal, federal appeal or Anders brief.
- Assign only supported errors consistent with the appellate filings and any amendment order. If a failure to rule is challenged, identify the opportunity given to the trial court to rule.
- Case files are not automatically the appellate record. Every record pinpoint, preservation statement and quoted passage must be checked against the actual lodged record.
- Final tables and word count depend on the completed document. The skeleton carries visible placeholders; it does not assert a measured word count or completed service.

## Presentation choices

An unnumbered centered cover, lower-Roman front matter and Arabic body beginning at 1 implement the published pagination scheme. Times New Roman 14 is an approved-font house choice; 14-point minimum and margins come from Rule 5A:4. Contents and authority entries remain editable placeholders pending final pagination. Headings are modest black text, and the body is double-spaced.

## Required case inputs

Appellate style/record number, lower court/judge, record-filing notice and orders, actual record/appendix, assignments and preservation citations, issues and authorities, requested disposition, counsel contacts, oral-argument election, measured final count and actual service.

## Verification and limits

Original clean drafting skeleton, with required sections and unresolved facts visible. Review of the blank template does not establish case-filled legal accuracy. Signatures, completed service, fees, enclosures and judicial acts must never be inferred from the request to draft.

The delivered artifact is an editable Word drafting template. Final PDF export, populated contents/authorities, bookmarks, PDF page labels, opening-view settings and the final word count still require verification before VACES filing. This library does not package a PDF filing or claim an unfilled Word skeleton is filing-ready. Keep the distinct cover, front-matter and body sections marked by `<!-- frontmatter -->` and `<!-- body -->`.
