## Requirements and primary authorities

- [RPC 1.18](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx) protects qualifying prospective-client information; a disclaimer does not erase duties created by the actual consultation.
- [RPC 1.1, 1.3, 1.4 and 1.6](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx) support competent intake, deadline triage, communication and confidentiality. No prescribed statewide PI intake form was identified.

## Conditional decisions

- Do not say the form alone conclusively prevents an attorney-client relationship. Evaluate actual communications.
- Avoid collecting SSNs, full account numbers or unnecessary sensitive records through an unsecured questionnaire.
- Government claims, minors, deceased persons and prior counsel need separate screening.

## Presentation choices

Single-spaced plain document with a descriptive title, short labeled sections and blank execution fields. No court caption or law-firm letter salutation; this is a readability choice, not a prescribed form.

## Required case inputs

Known client/contact details, incident information, involved parties, existing notices, treatment, benefits and evidence. Leave answers for the client or source-grounded extraction; do not fabricate an interview.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from the cited requirements; no commercial sample copied. Do not invent consent, service, signatures, facts, attachments, dates or completed transactions.
