# Plaintiff’s First Requests for Production to Defendant

Template: [rfp.md](../templates/discovery/rfp.md) · Word: [rfp.docx](../templates/discovery/docx/rfp.docx) · Shared choices: [common.md](common.md)

Verified: September 21, 2026.

**Scope:** Plaintiff’s party requests in Virginia Circuit Court motor-vehicle litigation; not a nonparty subpoena.

## Requirements and authorities

- [Rule 4:9(a), (b)](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf): reasonable particularity; reasonable time, place, period and manner; specific objections and withholding disclosure.
- [Rule 4:1(b)(3), (6), (7)](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf): work product, privilege descriptions and ESI limits.

## Conditional rules and case decisions

- Surveillance is not automatically discoverable in every circumstance; work-product protection and timing remain case-dependent.
- The 21-day/28-day response rules and court orders control; do not shorten production by inventing a deadline. Requests are ordinarily served, not filed.

## Presentation decisions

Six discrete starter requests. Native video is a chosen production specification, not a statewide format mandate.

See [shared formatting decisions](common.md): court requirements, observed caption convention and house style are separate.

## Required case inputs

Parties, event, relevant ranges, custody/control, realistic delivery details, requested formats, service and any discovery order.

## Use boundary

An editable drafting starting point. No facts, signature, completed service, consent, judicial ruling or attachment is established by a placeholder.
