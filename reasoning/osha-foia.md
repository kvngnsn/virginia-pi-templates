**Verified September 21, 2026.** Scope: Virginia DOLI/VOSH records request, with a separately selected federal OSHA alternative.

Read [shared decisions](common.md) alongside this file.

## Requirements and primary authorities

- [DOLI FOIA requests](https://doli.virginia.gov/resources/foia-requests/): identifies the Virginia agency process and existing-records limits.
- [Virginia Code § 2.2-3704](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/2.2-3704/): governs Virginia requester eligibility, procedure, fees and responses.
- [5 U.S.C. § 552](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/5/552): applies to the federal agency, with different procedures and exemptions.

## Conditional decisions

- Identify the custodian first. A Virginia worksite does not mean federal OSHA holds the investigation.
- Remove the unused jurisdictional branch. Do not promise a federal 20-day production deadline or a Virginia eligibility status that has not been verified.

## Presentation choices

Single-spaced block letter with paragraph spacing and a compact signature. No court caption or ornamental rules. These are presentation choices, not a prescribed form.

## Required case inputs

Custodian, inspection/site/dates, record categories, requester eligibility or fee category, cap and secure response details.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from requirements, not copied from a commercial form. No facts, signatures, service, consent, notarization, attachments, receipt of money or judicial acts may be invented.
