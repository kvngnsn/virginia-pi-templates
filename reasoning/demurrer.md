## Requirements and primary authorities

- [§ 8.01-273(A)](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title8.01/chapter7/section8.01-273/) requires a written demurrer stating specific grounds; grounds not specifically stated are not considered.
- [Rules 3:8(a), (a1), 1:8 and 4:15](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) govern responsiveness, serial pleading restrictions, amendments and hearing practice.

## Conditional decisions

- Do not treat the demurrer as an evidentiary summary-judgment motion or import a federal pleading standard without authority.
- Confirm filing sequence and leave; existing initial responses may trigger the current serial-pleading restriction.

## Presentation choices

Borderless parenthesis caption, black 12-point Times New Roman, one-inch margins, double spacing, restrained headings and page numbers; see common.md. These choices do not establish any local-court approval.

## Required case inputs

Complete target pleading, counts/paragraphs, actual filing/service history, precise defects, verified authorities and proposed disposition.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from the cited requirements; no commercial sample copied. Do not invent consent, service, signatures, facts, attachments, dates or completed transactions.
