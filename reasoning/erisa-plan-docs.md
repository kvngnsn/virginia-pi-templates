## Requirements and primary authorities

- [29 U.S.C. § 1024(b)(4)](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section1024&num=0&edition=prelim) defines the participant/beneficiary request to the administrator and specified documents.

## Conditional decisions

- Verify ERISA applicability, participant standing and the named administrator; an adjuster or recovery vendor may not be the administrator.
- Do not claim all claim notes or every historical record is automatically within subsection (b)(4), or that statutory penalties are automatic.
