## Requirements and source-supported constraints

Rule 7B:2 allows the General District Court to order a written bill of particulars and grounds of defense, with consequences for noncompliance or matters outside those pleadings.

## Conditional decisions and boundaries

Court scope is GENERAL_DISTRICT, not a renamed Circuit Court answer. The actual order controls filing/service time. Double spacing and the existing caption style are conservative house choices for this GDC draft, not a new local rule.

## Presentation choices

Use the established black 12-point Times New Roman, one-inch margins and editable Word fields. Font choice, modest headings and page footer are house choices unless a cited rule says otherwise.

## Required case inputs
