## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Virginia Code § 8.01-281](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-281/): subsection B permits separate trials; its specified motor-vehicle third-party scenario has a distinct mandatory provision.
- [Allstate Insurance Co. v. Wade, 265 Va. 383, 392–93 (2003)](https://www.vacourts.gov/opinions/opnscvwp/1021201.pdf): requires attention to prejudice, delay, expense and judicial resources.

## Conditional decisions

- Do not promise bifurcation as of right outside the statutory special case.
- No federal Rule 42 test is silently imported. Assess whether common factual issues require the same jury.
