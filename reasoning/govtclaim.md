# Virginia Tort Claims Act Notice reasoning

**Verified September 21, 2026.** Scope: Notice for a potentially cognizable VTCA claim against the Commonwealth or a transportation district; recipient depends on the agency.

Read [shared decisions](common.md) alongside this file.

## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Virginia Code § 8.01-195.6](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-195.6/): requires time, place, nature and agency identification, ordinarily within one year; receipt in the proper office controls filing.
- [Virginia Code § 8.01-195.3](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-195.3/): defines the waiver, limits and exceptions.

## Conditional decisions

- Select one actual statutory recipient and remove instructional alternatives before sending. Preserve proof of receipt, not just mailing.
- Do not use for local-government notice under § 15.2-209, federal FTCA, or a separate transportation entity without confirming the governing statute.
- The Act’s filing period, immunity exceptions and separate suit deadline still require review.

## Presentation choices

Single-spaced block letter with paragraph spacing and a compact signature. No court caption or ornamental rules. These are presentation choices, not a prescribed form.

## Required case inputs

Claimant/capacity, accrual, time/place/nature, exact responsible agency, proper official/address, receipt method and deadlines.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from requirements, not copied from a commercial form. No facts, signatures, service, consent, notarization, attachments, receipt of money or judicial acts may be invented.
