## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Rules 5:25 and 5A:18](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) require objections stated with reasonable certainty for appellate review, subject to their limited exceptions.
- [Rule 1:18 and Form 3 X](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) address instruction exchange/tender.

## Conditional decisions

- A blanket objection to every instruction is insufficiently informative; identify the exact error and remedy.
- A pretrial paper alone does not establish all steps needed for preservation; verify actual rulings and trial record.

## Presentation choices
