# Client Distribution Authorization and Record reasoning

Template: [client-check.md](../templates/settlement/client-check.md) · Word: [client-check.docx](../templates/settlement/docx/client-check.docx) · Shared choices: [common.md](common.md)

**Verified September 21, 2026.** Scope: Virginia settlement disbursement record; planned authorization, actual transfer and client acknowledgment kept separate.

Read [shared decisions](common.md) alongside this file.

## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Virginia RPC 1.15](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx): protects client/third-party funds and requires proper distribution and records.
- [Virginia RPC 1.4](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx): supports clear explanation and accurate status.

## Conditional decisions

- A generated document cannot execute a transfer. Never populate actual-transaction fields from planned payment data.
- Keep full bank details in a secure verified system. Independently verify changed instructions. Maintain protected reserves rather than treating client authorization as a waiver of third-party rights.

## Presentation choices

Single-spaced plain document with short labeled sections, readable execution fields and page numbers. No pleading caption. This is a house design, not a court-mandated form.

## Required case inputs

Cleared funds, reconciled accounting, authority, reserves, verified payee/instructions, actual transaction and actual receipt.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from requirements, not copied from a commercial form. No facts, signatures, service, consent, notarization, attachments, receipt of money or judicial acts may be invented.
