## Requirements and source-supported constraints

Rule 4:1(b)(4)(A)(i) authorizes interrogatories for trial-expert identity, subject, facts/opinions and grounds; (iv)-(vi) protect specified drafts/communications. Rules 4:8 and 4:1(e), plus actual Rule 1:18 orders, control responses, supplementation and scheduling.

## Conditional decisions and boundaries

Circuit Court civil discovery. Not an automatic federal Rule 26 exchange or California demand. Count all parts/subparts with prior interrogatories against Rule 4:8(g)’s 30 limit; normally serve without filing under 4:8(c). No unsupported demand for an expert report, protected draft or consultant.

## Presentation choices

Use the established black Times New Roman and one-inch Letter layout. The 12-point type, restrained headings and footer are house choices unless the cited rule governs a particular feature. Letters and agreements use single spacing; Circuit court papers use the established caption and double-spaced body.

## Required case inputs

Use actual case records, contracts, orders, authorizations and recipient instructions. Do not invent facts, authority, payments, attachments, consent, service, signatures, specialist work or agency action. Bracketed fields remain unresolved until supported. Verify any legal or record citation added while drafting for the specific case.
