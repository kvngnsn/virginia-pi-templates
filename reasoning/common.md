# Shared Virginia template decisions

Verified September 21, 2026 in the source snapshot. This file records the design rationale shared by every template. Per-template files in this folder add the specific requirements, conditional decisions and sources.

## Court requirements

- [Rules 1:4, 1:5](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf): essential pleading facts, numbered paragraphs as applicable, signature and counsel details. [§ 8.01-271.1](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title8.01/chapter7/section8.01-271.1/) also governs counsel signatures and factual/legal inquiry.
- [Rule 1:16(a)(1)](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf): US Letter and double-spaced typed material for filings, except quotations, subject to electronic-filing provisions. The court layout double-spaces caption and signature text too. For served but unfiled discovery, double spacing is a consistency choice, not an asserted separate filing mandate.
- [Rules 1:12, 1:17](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf): actual authorized service; date/method certification where applicable. Email does not automatically qualify merely because an address is known. Rule 1:12 includes a separate mail/fax certificate requirement for its email route; check the applicable electronic-case rule. No certificate is pre-completed.
- [Rules 1:15, 4:15](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf): local procedures and motion setting/notice must be checked. The generic templates do not supply local clearance.

## Observed convention

Part Five Appendix Form 1 (PDF p. 445) illustrates a circuit-court caption with party names at left, closing parentheses between columns and the case number at right. It is an official example of that presentation, not a mandatory complaint form or measured evidence of the most common layout. Part One's praecipe (PDF p. 44) uses a different arrangement.

## Design choices (house style, not rules)

Black 12-point Times New Roman; one-inch margins; borderless three-column caption; modest bold headings; editable paragraph numbering; bottom-center page numbers. Font and margin values are house choices, not requirements attributed to Rule 1:16. Letters use block format, single spacing and paragraph gaps, with no court caption. A template's title identifies its actual purpose. Appellate briefs and papers use at least 14-point type (see [layout profiles](../references/layout-profiles.md)).

## Content controls

Use case-supported facts. Leave unknowns visible. Do not manufacture service, signatures, notarization, conferral, consent, exhibits, production, injuries, damages or legal deadlines. Request numbering and text in responses must come from the actual served documents.

## Source provenance

Rules were checked against the [official compilation](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) and [amendment index](https://www.vacourts.gov/courts/scv/amend). Template wording was drafted from requirements, not copied from a commercial pleading or form.
