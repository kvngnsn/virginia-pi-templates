## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Virginia Code § 8.01-232](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-232/): requires an accrued claim, settlement-deferral purpose, signed written promise and filing within the earlier statutory or agreed period; B–D require separate attention.

## Conditional decisions

- Do not describe this as simply stopping and restarting every clock. Compute the statutory outside limit and choose an enforceable deadline.
- Public construction procurement under subsection D is outside this template. Estate, joint-contractor, repose and claim-notice issues require separate analysis.

## Presentation choices

## Required case inputs

Accrual, original deadline, precise claim, parties, applicable period, date promise made, earlier agreed deadline and actual authority.
