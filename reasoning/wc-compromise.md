## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Virginia Code § 65.2-309](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/65.2-309/): creates subrogation/lien rights, specifies settlement protections and other procedures.
- [Virginia Code § 65.2-310](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/65.2-310/): addresses ascertainment of compensation amounts and payment from a recovery.
- [Virginia Code § 65.2-311](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/65.2-311/): provides proportional reasonable expenses and fees in the specified circumstances.
- [Virginia Code § 65.2-313](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/65.2-313/): current 2026 text governs future-entitlement accounting and continuing payments; a credit alone cannot suspend an award.

## Conditional decisions

- Separate the accrued lien, fee/cost allocation, future credit and continuing benefits. No automatic one-third reduction or blanket benefits holiday is assumed.
- Identify the actual employer, carrier and Commission awards; obtain legally required approvals before representing a settlement as effective.
- A third-party lien compromise is not necessarily a Commission-approved compromise of the employee’s compensation claim. Do not combine them silently.

## Presentation choices

Single-spaced block letter with paragraph spacing and a compact signature. No court caption or ornamental rules. These are presentation choices, not a prescribed form.

## Required case inputs

Verified lien, disputed entries, fee/cost calculation, additional concession, gross recovery, future treatment and authority.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from requirements, not copied from a commercial form. No facts, signatures, service, consent, notarization, attachments, receipt of money or judicial acts may be invented.
