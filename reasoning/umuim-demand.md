## Requirements and primary authorities

- [§ 38.2-2206](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title38.2/chapter22/section38.2-2206/) governs coverage and relevant settlement/process issues; policy timing and elections must be checked.
- [RPC 1.2(a), 1.4 and 4.1](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx) require authorized and supportable demands.

## Conditional decisions

- Do not hard-code liability-limit offsets, policy stacking, exhaustion or a consent requirement without the actual governing regime.
- Unknown-driver prerequisites, liability settlements and released-defendant litigation may need separate statutory documents.

## Presentation choices

Black 12-point Times New Roman, one-inch margins and single spacing. Block letter format with paragraph gaps.

## Required case inputs

Policy and elections, coverage status, adverse coverage, other payments, actual releases/notices/service, proof of liability and damages, authorized demand and terms.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from the cited requirements; no commercial sample copied. Do not invent consent, service, signatures, facts, attachments, dates or completed transactions.
