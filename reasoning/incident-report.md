# Voluntary Request for Incident Records reasoning

Template: [incident-report.md](../templates/investigation/incident-report.md) · Word: [incident-report.docx](../templates/investigation/docx/incident-report.docx) · Shared choices: [common.md](common.md)

**Verified September 21, 2026.** Scope: Pre-suit request to a private premises owner or business about a specific incident; no automatic FOIA or compulsory-production power.

Read [shared decisions](common.md) alongside this file.

## Requirements and primary authorities

- [§ 8.01-379.2:1](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title8.01/chapter13/section8.01-379.2:1/) governs preservation when litigation is reasonably foreseeable.
- [Rules 4:9 and 4:9A](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) distinguish party discovery and nonparty production once proper procedures are available.

## Conditional decisions

- Preservation duty is fact-dependent; the request itself is not a court finding of spoliation.
- Privilege, privacy, other custodians and compelled production require separate analysis.

## Presentation choices

Black 12-point Times New Roman, one-inch margins and single spacing. Block letter format with paragraph gaps.

## Required case inputs

Incident, custodian, bounded dates/topics, records sought, retention risk and secure delivery.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from the cited requirements; no commercial sample copied. Do not invent consent, service, signatures, facts, attachments, dates or completed transactions.
