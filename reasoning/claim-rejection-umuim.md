## Requirements and source-supported constraints

§ 38.2-2206(H) prohibits requiring arbitration in covered Virginia UM/UIM endorsements; subsection F has distinct insurer service requirements. A generic arbitration demand would misstate Virginia rights.

## Conditional decisions and boundaries

Original dispute/reconsideration letter with an expressly voluntary arbitration proposal. It does not compel arbitration, create consent or preserve a deadline. If the client does not authorize proposing arbitration, use the ordinary UM/UIM claim correspondence instead.
