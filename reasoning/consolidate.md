## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Allstate Insurance Co. v. Wade, 265 Va. 383, 392 (2003)](https://www.vacourts.gov/opinions/opnscvwp/1021201.pdf): recognizes trial-court discretion over consolidation and separation.
- [Virginia Code § 8.01-267.1](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-267.1/): sets six-plaintiff and other conditions for the Multiple Claimant Litigation Act.
- [Virginia Code § 8.01-267.3](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/8.01-267.3/): governs qualifying actions pending in the same court.

## Conditional decisions

- Ordinary related-case consolidation and the Multiple Claimant Litigation Act are separate routes. The six-plaintiff statute is not a universal prerequisite.
- Different-court transfers require their own authority and procedure; this template does not accomplish one.

## Presentation choices

Double-spaced court profile with the established borderless parenthesis caption, modest headings and editable numbering. Signature and service blocks are kept together. Caption geometry, Times New Roman 12 and one-inch margins are house choices; the court-paper spacing requirement is distinguished in common.md.

## Required case inputs

All case captions, same-court status, overlap, prejudice, positions, appropriate authority and precise coordinated procedure.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from requirements, not copied from a commercial form. No facts, signatures, service, consent, notarization, attachments, receipt of money or judicial acts may be invented.
