## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Rules 4:1(b)(4) and 2:702](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) distinguish expert discovery and admissibility questions.
- [Rule 3.4(c)](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx) restricts compensation contingent on a witness giving favorable testimony.

## Conditional decisions

- Separate consulting from testifying role; do not promise universal work-product protection.
- Projected care is a supported opinion, not an invented medical recommendation or guaranteed future expense.

## Presentation choices

Block letter with single spacing and paragraph gaps; no court caption. Black 12-point Times New Roman, one-inch margins and page numbers are house choices; this does not establish local-court approval. Keep the final document's legal purpose distinct from the command's legacy label.
