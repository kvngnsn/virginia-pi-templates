## Requirements and primary authorities

- [§ 8.01-412.2](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title8.01/chapter14/section8.01-412.2/) permits audiovisual recording and simultaneous records/copies; [§ 8.01-412.3](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title8.01/chapter14/section8.01-412.3/) requires notice in both the notice and attendance subpoena.
- [Rules 4:5 and 4:7A](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) govern logistics, on-camera procedures, editing and use.

## Conditional decisions

- Do not treat remote conferencing, audiovisual recording and stenographic transcription as the same fact.
- A signed stipulation or actual order is required for editing under Rule 4:7A(c); preserve the original.
