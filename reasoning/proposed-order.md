**Verified September 21, 2026.** Scope: Proposed Virginia Circuit Court ruling on a specified issue; not an entered order.

Read [shared decisions](common.md) alongside this file.

## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Rule 1:13](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) requires counsel endorsement or reasonable presentation notice with the draft, subject to court modification/dispensation.
- [Rule 1:1](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) makes accurate finality and entry information consequential; a draft does not itself start an entered-order clock.

## Conditional decisions

- Recitals of a hearing, findings and rulings must match what actually occurred or what is expressly proposed.
- Do not add generic final-order language that accidentally disposes of remaining claims.

## Presentation choices

Borderless parenthesis caption, black 12-point Times New Roman, one-inch margins, double spacing, restrained headings and page numbers; see common.md. These choices do not establish any local-court approval.

## Required case inputs

Motion/issue, actual ruling or proposed disposition, precise acts/deadlines, affected parties, remaining claims and endorsement/presentation status.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted from the cited requirements; no commercial sample copied. Do not invent consent, service, signatures, facts, attachments, dates or completed transactions.
