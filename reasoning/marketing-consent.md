## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Rules 1.6, 1.4 and 7.1](https://vsb.org/Site/about/RulesRegulations/rpc-part6-sec2.aspx) govern informed disclosure of client information and truthful, nonmisleading advertising.

## Conditional decisions

- Approval must identify the actual content and channels; do not infer permission from a favorable outcome or public docket.
- Separate settlement confidentiality, image rights and third-party information; consent does not cure misleading claims.

## Presentation choices

Standalone document with single spacing, restrained headings and explicit fields; no court caption. Keep the final document's legal purpose distinct from the command's legacy label.

## Required case inputs

Exact content, identity exposure, channels/duration, confidentiality restrictions, any benefit and withdrawal process.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted for this purpose; no commercial template copied. Signatures, service, consent, attachments, transactions and facts must reflect actual events. Confirm current law and the entered case orders before use.
