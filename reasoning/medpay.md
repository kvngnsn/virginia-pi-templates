## Requirements and source-supported constraints

Virginia motor-vehicle MedPay anti-subrogation law must be checked before recognizing a recovery claim. Its statutory policy/vehicle conditions matter.

## Conditional decisions and boundaries

The label MedPay/PIP does not establish the governing law or a lien. /medpay-notice is the same recovery-notice purpose in the existing template records. Do not apply self-funded ERISA or out-of-state PIP rules by assumption.
