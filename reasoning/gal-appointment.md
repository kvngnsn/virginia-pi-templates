## Requirements and source-supported constraints

§ 8.01-9(A) addresses defendants under disability, while (B) recognizes representation by counsel and interests-of-justice/statutory exceptions. § 8.01-8 permits a minor to sue by next friend. Do not assume every minor plaintiff needs a separate GAL.

## Conditional decisions and boundaries

Circuit Court appointment motion only. The original catalog combined appointment and report; an actual GAL must investigate and author the report. Do not generate findings, consent, a completed appointment or unsupported mandatory appointment.
