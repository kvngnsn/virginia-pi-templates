## Requirements and source-supported constraints

Preserve source attribution, confidential information and the distinction between factual chronology and legally computed deadlines.

## Conditional decisions and boundaries

This is a case-analysis chronology, not an automatic filing or a guarantee of calendared deadlines.

## Presentation choices

Use the established black 12-point Times New Roman, one-inch margins and editable Word fields. Block letters and internal agreements/summaries use single spacing; court papers use the established double-spaced caption/body. Font choice, modest headings and page footer are house choices unless a cited rule says otherwise.

## Required case inputs

Resolve each bracketed field against actual case documents, authorizations, orders and recipient instructions. Do not invent dates, fees, payment, consent, signatures, service, attachments or agency action. Retain unknowns for attorney completion. Any authority or record citation inserted during drafting must be verified for that case.
