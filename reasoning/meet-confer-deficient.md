# Discovery Deficiency and Conference Request

Verified: September 21, 2026.

**Scope:** Good-faith correspondence concerning Virginia Circuit Court civil discovery.

## Requirements and authorities

- Good-faith conferral or an attempt is required for the pertinent discovery motions. [Rules 4:1(b)(8), 4:1(c), 4:9(b)(ii), 4:12(a)(2)](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf).

## Conditional rules and case decisions

- Sending one letter does not automatically prove the required good-faith effort. Preserve actual communications and responses.
- A proposed cure date does not amend a court deadline. Analyze the correct underlying discovery rule and any orders.

## Presentation decisions

Letter organized by request, response, defect and cure. No caption or false certificate that a conference occurred.

See [shared formatting decisions](common.md): court requirements, observed caption convention and house style are separate.

## Required case inputs

Actual served set/responses, disputed items, accurate timeline, intended narrow cure and genuine conference availability.

## Use boundary

An editable drafting starting point. No facts, signature, completed service, consent, judicial ruling or attachment is established by a placeholder.
