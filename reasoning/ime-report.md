## Requirements and primary authorities

- [Rule 4:10(c)](https://www.vacourts.gov/static/courts/scv/rulesofcourt.pdf) governs detailed report content, filing/copies and agreed examinations.

## Conditional decisions

- Use the actual order/agreement; do not invent a universal number of days after examination.
- Report receipt and an examiner deposition are distinct acts with different potential privilege consequences.

## Presentation choices

Block letter with single spacing and paragraph gaps; no court caption. Black 12-point Times New Roman, one-inch margins and page numbers are house choices; this does not establish local-court approval. Keep the final document's legal purpose distinct from the command's legacy label.

## Required case inputs

Examiner, actual examination date, order/agreement, report deadline and known filing/delivery status.

## Scope and verification limits

Original wording drafted for this purpose; no commercial template copied. Signatures, service, consent, attachments, transactions and facts must reflect actual events. Confirm current law and the entered case orders before use.
