## Requirements and source-supported constraints

Virginia Judicial Settlement Conference Program instructions: after judicial referral attendance is required, but agreement to settle is not. Confidentiality is subject to independently admissible material and actual governing instructions.

## Conditional decisions and boundaries

Circuit Court civil conference under an actual referral or authorized procedure. The original mandatory-settlement label is not a universal filing requirement. Use the actual neutral’s exchange/confidentiality rules; do not add a routine service certificate or claim blanket privilege.

## Required case inputs

Use actual case records, contracts, orders, authorizations and recipient instructions.
