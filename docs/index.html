    How to use these templates

    1. Pick the template by purpose and court. Each template states its scope. Court papers marked “Virginia Circuit Court” are drafted for that court only.
    2. Fill only supported facts. Replace a bracketed field only with a fact from the case file, an order, or counsel. Leave anything unknown in brackets.
    3. Never invent facts, dates, amounts, citations, service, signatures, consent or deadlines. Check any citation you add.
    4. Keep the layout profile. Court papers are double-spaced with a caption; letters and plain documents are single-spaced; appellate briefs use at least 14-point type with cover, front matter and body sections.
    5. Have a Virginia attorney review the draft before it is sent, signed or filed.

    Line format

    Template text is line-oriented: # Title and ## Heading lines, 1. numbered paragraphs, caption rows split by | (left party text, right case number), and markers <!-- pagebreak -->, <!-- frontmatter --> and <!-- body -->. The Word files already apply this layout. To turn a filled draft into a .docx, use scripts/fill_template.py from the repository.
