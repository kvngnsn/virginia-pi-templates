## Recipient protections
If compliance with this attorney-issued subpoena is required less than 14 days after service, you may serve the issuing party with written objections stating your grounds. Upon such objection, production may be compelled only by court order. Rule 4:9A(c) also provides procedures to seek prompt protection from undue burden or expense, to address inaccessible electronic sources, and to request appropriate cost allocation. Nonparty financial or attorney-client protected records receive the protections of § 8.01-420.9 where applicable.
