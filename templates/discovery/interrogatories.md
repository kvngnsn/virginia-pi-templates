# PLAINTIFF’S FIRST INTERROGATORIES TO DEFENDANT
Plaintiff [NAME] serves these interrogatories on Defendant [NAME] under Rules 4:1 and 4:8. “Incident” means the [DATE] motor-vehicle collision at [LOCATION]. Answer within the time applicable under Rule 4:8(d), subject to any valid extension or order.
## Interrogatories
1. Identify each person known to you to have witnessed the Incident.
2. Describe your account of how the Incident occurred.
3. State the facts supporting your contention, if any, that Plaintiff's conduct contributed to the Incident.
4. Identify each person, other than Plaintiff, whose conduct you contend caused or contributed to the Incident.
5. Identify photographs, recordings or electronically stored information depicting the Incident that are within your possession, custody or control.
6. Identify any person who investigated the Incident on your behalf, excluding information protected from disclosure.
7. Identify the insurance agreements described in Rule 4:1(b)(2) that may satisfy all or part of a judgment in this action.
8. Identify each statement by Plaintiff concerning the Incident within your possession, custody or control.
Each answer must restate the interrogatory and respond separately under oath. State specific objections and answer any unobjectionable portion as Rule 4:8 requires.
