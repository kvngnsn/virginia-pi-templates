## Limits and reserved matters
This promise applies only to the stated parties and claim. It does not revive a claim already barred when the promise was made, bind another person, waive a statute of repose, extend a notice-of-claim or appeal deadline, or establish liability. All other defenses are reserved except as expressly stated: [ANY NEGOTIATED EXCEPTION].
