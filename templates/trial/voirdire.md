# PROPOSED VOIR DIRE QUESTIONS
[PARTY] submits the following proposed questions for examination of prospective jurors, subject to the Court's procedure.
## Connections and knowledge
1. Do you know [PARTIES, COUNSEL OR IDENTIFIED WITNESSES], or have a relationship with any of them that may affect your ability to decide this case fairly?
2. Do you have personal knowledge of [IDENTIFIED INCIDENT], or have you received information about this dispute outside this courtroom?
3. Do you have any financial or other interest in the outcome of this case?
## Case-related impartiality
4. Have you had an experience involving [NEUTRALLY DESCRIBED, RELEVANT SUBJECT] that may affect your ability to consider the evidence impartially?
5. Have you formed an opinion about [CASE-SPECIFIC ISSUE DESCRIBED WITHOUT ARGUMENT] that would make it difficult to follow the Court's instructions?
6. Is there any reason you could not consider this case fairly on the evidence and instructions?
