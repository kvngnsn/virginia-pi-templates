This authorization expires on [SPECIFIC DATE OR EVENT RELATED TO THE PATIENT OR PURPOSE]. I may revoke it at any time by sending a written revocation to [RECORD HOLDER CONTACT AND ADDRESS]. Revocation does not affect action already taken in reliance on this authorization.
## My choices and understanding

I understand that information disclosed to the recipient may be disclosed again and may no longer be protected by HIPAA. This does not override applicable state-law limits on redisclosure.
I may keep a copy of this signed authorization. This document authorizes only the disclosure described above; it does not authorize settlement or release legal claims.
