## Covered information and permitted use
“Confidential Information” means [PRECISELY IDENTIFIED MATERIALS AND DESIGNATION METHOD] disclosed for the stated purpose. The receiving party may use it only for that purpose and disclose it only to [DEFINED REPRESENTATIVES] who need access and are subject to appropriate confidentiality duties.
Information is excluded to the extent it was lawfully known without restriction, becomes public without breach, is independently developed, or is lawfully received from another source without a confidentiality duty.
## Legal obligations and protection
The receiving party will use [AGREED REASONABLE SAFEGUARDS]. If disclosure is required by law or process, it may disclose what is legally required, providing lawful advance notice where permitted and a reasonable opportunity to seek protection. No provision restricts legally protected reporting, cooperation with authorities, truthful testimony or compliance with professional duties.
This agreement creates no court sealing order and changes no discovery obligation. It does not establish privilege for otherwise unprotected material or constitute a release of claims.

The duties continue for [DEFINED PERIOD AND ANY LAWFUL EXCEPTION]. On [TRIGGER], materials will be [RETURNED OR DESTROYED], subject to identified legal retention, backup and counsel-record exceptions with continued protection.
