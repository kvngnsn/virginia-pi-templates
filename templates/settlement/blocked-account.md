# PROPOSED ORDER FOR PROTECTED DEPOSIT
This matter came before the Court on [MOTION AND HEARING DATE]. The Court considered [ACTUAL EVIDENCE AND NOTICE]. The compromise was approved in [ORDER DATE AND REFERENCE].
1. The net sum of [AMOUNT] belongs to [MINOR NAME]. Under Virginia Code § 8.01-424(D)(3), payment shall be made to [DULY QUALIFIED FIDUCIARY AND CAPACITY], after the Court’s inquiry into the adequacy of the fiduciary’s bond. Qualification and bond are established by [RECORD AND FINDINGS].
2. The fiduciary shall deposit the sum at [INSTITUTION] in an account titled [FIDUCIARY CAPACITY AND BENEFICIARY]. The institution’s written acceptance of the ordered restrictions shall be obtained before transfer. The complete account number shall be maintained through [AUTHORIZED NONPUBLIC RECORD].
3. No withdrawal, pledge, transfer or change of beneficial ownership is authorized except upon further written order of this Court. Interest shall remain in the account. The fiduciary shall obtain statements and preserve the funds subject to all applicable fiduciary duties.
4. By [DATE], the fiduciary shall provide proof of deposit and the institution’s acknowledgment through [REQUIRED FILING OR DELIVERY METHOD]. Required inventories and accountings remain due to [PROPER AUTHORITY]; this Order does not excuse them.
5. Attainment of majority does not itself authorize the bank to disregard this Order. Distribution and discharge shall occur pursuant to [SPECIFIC FURTHER-ORDER PROCEDURE], consistent with the beneficiary’s rights and applicable law.
