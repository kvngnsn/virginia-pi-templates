## Limited undertaking
With the client's authorization below, [FIRM] agrees, upon actual receipt of recovery funds for this matter, to protect the provider's properly supported claim for [IDENTIFIED SERVICES AND DATES], subject to applicable law, superior interests, the client's rights and any written compromise. The current itemized balance is [AMOUNT AND STATEMENT DATE].
This undertaking concerns funds actually received and legally available for the claim. It is not a guarantee of a recovery, a promise by the firm to pay from its own funds, or an admission that every charge or asserted lien is valid. No unrelated treatment or future charge is included without a further written agreement.
The provider agrees to [PRECISE BILLING, COLLECTION-FORBEARANCE AND NOTICE TERMS]. Any reduction or satisfaction of the patient's remaining debt must be separately stated in writing. A limitation on a statutory lien does not itself eliminate the underlying bill.
If entitlement is disputed, the disputed portion will be held and handled as required by law and professional obligations while the dispute is resolved. Undisputed funds will not be withheld merely to obtain a concession.
