# AFFIDAVIT CONCERNING AN UNKNOWN MOTORIST
I, [AFFIANT NAME], being duly sworn, state:
1. I am [AFFIANT NAME], [ROLE AND BASIS OF PERSONAL KNOWLEDGE]. On [DATE AND TIME] at [LOCATION], I was [DESCRIBE ACTIVITY AND VEHICLE].
2. The occurrence was [PERSONAL OBSERVATIONS OF THE OTHER VEHICLE, CONDUCT AND RESULTING INCIDENT]. Physical contact [DID / DID NOT] occur as follows: [SPECIFIC FACTS].
3. The other vehicle’s driver, owner and identifying information known to me are [FACTS; DISTINGUISH UNKNOWN FROM UNCONFIRMED]. Efforts actually made to identify them were [STEPS, DATES AND RESULTS].
4. I reported the occurrence to [INSURER OR LAW-ENFORCEMENT OFFICER] on [DATE, TIME AND METHOD], reference [NUMBER]. [STATE FACTS EXPLAINING THE TIMING OF ANY REPORT].
5. Witnesses and supporting materials known to me are [NAMES, CONTACT METHOD, REPORTS AND ACTUAL ATTACHMENTS]. I do not claim personal knowledge of facts learned only from others; those sources are [IDENTIFY].
The foregoing is true and correct to the best of my personal knowledge as stated.
Signature: ____________________
Printed name: [AFFIANT NAME]
COMMONWEALTH / STATE OF [STATE]
CITY / COUNTY OF [LOCALITY]
Subscribed and sworn to before me on [ACTUAL DATE] by [AFFIANT NAME].
